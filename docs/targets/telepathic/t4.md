## RV Target Sheet: 5192-4088

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 5192-4088
*   **Historical Identity:** Howard Carter
*   **Time Period:** November 26, 1922, late afternoon (around 4:00 PM).
*   **Event / Timeline:** The moment of making the first small breach in the sealed doorway of Tutankhamun's tomb (KV62) and looking inside.
*   **Location:** Subterranean corridor in the Valley of the Kings, near Luxor, Egypt.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A man of average height, with a wiry, hardened physique from decades of physical labor in the desert. 
*   **Face:** Deeply tanned, weather-beaten, and covered in a fine layer of pale limestone dust. He sports a thick, dark mustache. 
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Dark, receding hair, slicked back with sweat. Dark, intensely focused eyes straining to see in the darkness.
*   **Attire:** Dressed in a practical but filthy outfit typical of 1920s field archaeology. A white (now gray and yellowed with dust) linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves, suspenders, and durable trousers. His clothes are heavily stained with sweat and earth.

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** Extreme, almost unbearable anticipation mixed with the dread of potential disappointment. This is the absolute climax of his life's work. He is acutely aware that this is his final chance—his wealthy sponsor was about to cut funding after years of finding nothing. 
*   **Emotions:** A powerful surge of adrenaline, disbelief, and awe. The overwhelming fear that the tomb might have been emptied by ancient robbers is suddenly replaced by the shock of discovering an intact hoard. 
*   **Behavior:** His hands are trembling so violently that he struggles to hold his light source steady. He uses an iron testing rod to pry a small hole in the ancient plaster. He leans in closely, inserting a lit candle. He remains completely silent and paralyzed for what seems like an eternity to the people behind him. When asked if he can see anything, he finally whispers in a breathless, awe-struck voice: "Yes, wonderful things."

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** A steep, narrow, subterranean corridor carved out of solid limestone bedrock. It is a dead-end tunnel blocked by a plastered wall bearing the stamped seals of a necropolis guard.
*   **Interior:** Claustrophobic, cramped, and entirely devoid of natural light. The space immediately behind him is tightly packed with a few other anxious individuals (Lord Carnarvon, his daughter, and an assistant). 
*   **Visual Stimuli:** Pitch blackness, broken only by the small, warm, and highly unstable, flickering yellow light of a candle. As his eyes slowly adjust to the dark through the small hole, chaotic glints of gold emerge from the shadows. The shapes of strange, gilded animal statues (couches), alabaster vases, and the metallic gleam of treasures gradually materialize out of the gloom.
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** The harsh, metallic scraping of the iron rod against dry stone and brittle plaster. The echoing, hushed whispers and heavy, anxious breathing of the people standing directly behind him in the tight corridor.
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** A sudden, distinct rush of hot air escaping from the pierced chamber. It carries an incredibly heavy, ancient smell—a mix of dry rot, millennia-old dust, evaporated essential oils, resins, and the dead, stagnant air that has been sealed away for over 3,000 years.
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** Oppressive, suffocating heat and stale air inside the tunnel. The abrasive texture of fine limestone dust coating his skin, getting into his eyes and throat. The heat of the candle wax near his trembling fingers, and the physical sensation of the warm breeze blowing out from the tomb's interior against his face.

### Social Standing
*   The man is a brilliant, self-taught English archaeologist and artist who lacks the formal university education of his peers, often making him an outsider looked down upon by the academic establishment. He is fiercely stubborn and entirely dependent on the financial backing of a wealthy aristocrat. In this exact moment, he transforms from a frustrated, nearly-defeated excavator into the man who has just made the most spectacular and famous archaeological discovery in human history.
