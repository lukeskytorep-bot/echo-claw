## RV Target Sheet: 2634-7119

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 2634-7119
*   **Historical Identity:** Marie Curie (Maria Skłodowska-Curie)
*   **Time Period:** A late evening in 1902.
*   **Event / Timeline:** Returning to the laboratory shed at night to witness the first successfully isolated, visible amount of pure radium salts glowing in the dark.
*   **Location:** A dilapidated, uninsulated wooden shed acting as a laboratory at the School of Physics and Chemistry in Paris, France.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A petite, slender woman. Her posture is slightly stooped, reflecting immense physical exhaustion from years of grueling manual labor (moving heavy sacks of ore and stirring boiling vats).
*   **Face:** Pale, serious, and prematurely aged by stress, chemical exposure, and malnutrition. High cheekbones and a broad forehead. 
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Ash-blonde hair pulled back tightly into a practical, no-nonsense bun. Light, penetrating eyes that convey an unyielding, intense focus.
*   **Attire:** She wears a simple, dark, floor-length Victorian dress, completely devoid of decorative elements. Over the dress, she wears a heavy, coarse linen laboratory apron that is stiff with dirt, permanently stained by harsh acids, and covered in metallic dust. Her hands are dry, severely cracked, and marked by strange, slow-healing burns (early signs of severe radiation damage, though unrecognized at the time).

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** A state of quiet, profound triumph mixed with physical exhaustion. She possesses a singular, relentless obsession and an iron-willed determination that allowed her to endure four years of seemingly impossible, repetitive labor.
*   **Emotions:** Deep, almost maternal tenderness and awe toward the substance she has managed to extract. A profound sense of scientific reverence. Despite her physical pain and fatigue, she is experiencing a moment of pure, transcendent joy and intellectual vindication.
*   **Behavior:** She stands in the darkness of the shed alongside her husband, completely silent. She reaches out with her scarred hands to gently touch the glass tubes containing the substance. Her movements are careful, deliberate, and deeply respectful, as if handling a sacred relic.

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** An abandoned, miserable wooden shack originally used as a medical dissection room. It has a dirt floor, drafty wooden walls, and a patched glass skylight that leaks terribly during the rain.
*   **Interior:** Cramped and chaotic. The space is filled with rough wooden workbenches, heavy iron cauldrons, mortar and pestles, and hundreds of delicate glass crystallizing bowls in various stages of evaporation.
*   **Visual Stimuli:** The space is wrapped in the deep darkness of a Parisian night, but it is not lit by gas or candle. Instead, the room is illuminated by a faint, ethereal, pale blue-green luminescence. The light emanates directly from several small glass tubes and bowls scattered across the tables. It is a "fairy-like," ghostly glow that casts soft, alien shadows on the rough wooden walls.
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** The quiet rustle of her heavy wool and linen clothing. The faint dripping of condensation from the glass roof onto the dirt floor. The hushed, awestruck whispers shared between a man and a woman in the dark. From outside, the distant, muffled sound of horse-drawn carriages on Parisian cobblestones.
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** A harsh, toxic atmosphere. The air is heavy with the pungent, acrid stench of hydrogen sulfide, concentrated hydrochloric and sulfuric acids, damp earth, and the metallic dust of pitchblende ore. 
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** The biting, damp cold of the unheated shed chilling her to the bone. The smooth, cold surface of the glass tubes contrasting with the rough, painful, and inflamed skin of her damaged fingertips. A deep, lingering muscular ache in her back and shoulders from years of stirring heavy, boiling liquids with a heavy iron rod.

### Social Standing
*   The woman is a Polish immigrant in a deeply conservative, male-dominated French academic world. She is working entirely outside the established scientific hierarchy, receiving no salary for her laboratory work and surviving on a meager budget. She is often dismissed or overshadowed by her male peers. Yet, in this exact moment, standing in a freezing, toxic shed, she holds in her hands the physical proof of a discovery that will shatter the foundations of classical physics, ultimately earning her two Nobel Prizes and changing the modern world forever.
