## RV Target Sheet: 5392-1044

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 5392-1044
*   **Historical Identity:** Mahatma (Mohandas Karamchand) Gandhi
*   **Time Period:** April 6, 1930, early morning (around 6:30 AM).
*   **Event / Timeline:** The climax of the Salt March (Dandi March). The exact moment he bends down and picks up a lump of natural sea salt from the mud, deliberately breaking the British Empire's Salt Act and sparking a massive, non-violent revolution.
*   **Location:** The muddy shoreline of the Arabian Sea at the village of Dandi, Gujarat, British India.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A short man (approx. 164 cm / 5'4") who appears incredibly frail, thin, and physically weak (weighing around 49 kg / 108 lbs). He relies on a long, thick bamboo walking stick for support.
*   **Face:** A gaunt face with a prominent nose, large ears, and a warm, toothless smile. He wears iconic, round, wire-rimmed spectacles.
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Completely bald. His dark eyes project a profound, luminous gentleness mixed with an unyielding, immovable focus. 
*   **Attire:** Dressed in extreme, deliberate poverty. He wears only a simple, rough, white loincloth (*dhoti*) made of hand-spun cotton (*khadi*) and a matching shawl draped over his shoulders. His chest, arms, and wiry legs are bare. He is barefoot in the wet mud.

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** A state of absolute, crystalline inner peace and spiritual clarity (*Satyagraha* – "truth-force"). He possesses an unbreakable, quiet certainty. His mind is entirely free of panic, pride, or aggressive ambition.
*   **Emotions:** A profound, radical absence of hatred or malice, even toward his oppressors. He operates from a place of active, expansive love and empathy. He is fully aware that this tiny, simple physical gesture will ignite a subcontinent and result in his own imprisonment, yet he feels a deep, joyful liberation.
*   **Behavior:** His movements are slow, deliberate, and highly ritualistic. After bathing in the ocean, he walks calmly up the beach. Surrounded by absolute tension, he moves without hesitation, bending his stiff, 60-year-old knees to reach the ground. He pinches a crust of muddy salt between his fingers, holds it up for the crowd to see, and does so with total, silent dignity.

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** An open, flat, boundless expanse of coastal salt flats leading directly into the vast ocean. There are no walls or ceilings; the environment is defined by horizontal lines of water, mud, and sky.
*   **Interior:** N/A (Outdoors).
*   **Visual Stimuli:** The soft, golden, low-angled light of the early morning sun rising over the water, casting long shadows. A massive, densely packed crowd of thousands of followers, uniformly dressed in white homespun cloth, stands behind him. On the ground, the stark gray and white crust of evaporated sea salt mixed with dark, wet beach mud.
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** The rhythmic, gentle, and indifferent crashing of the ocean waves against the shoreline. This is contrasted by the extraordinary, highly charged, and heavy silence of thousands of people holding their collective breath in anticipation. The soft, wet *squelch* of his bare feet pressing into the coastal mud.
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** The sharp, clean, briny smell of the ocean breeze. The scent of wet sand, drying salt, and seaweed, mixed with the faint, warm smell of sweat, cotton, and incense emanating from the massive crowd gathered just yards away.
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** The cool, damp morning breeze brushing against his bare chest and legs. The gritty, wet, rough texture of the muddy salt crust rubbing between his thumb and forefinger. A deep, pervasive physical exhaustion vibrating in his bones—the result of having just walked 240 miles (385 km) on foot over 24 days—which is entirely overridden by a massive surge of spiritual energy and willpower.

### Social Standing
*   The man is a living paradox. Officially, he is a disbarred lawyer with zero wealth, no military rank, no weapons, and no formal political office. He lives by choice as the poorest of the poor. Yet, he is revered as a "Mahatma" (Great Soul) by hundreds of millions of people. At this exact moment, this frail, half-naked old man picking up a pinch of mud holds more genuine, raw moral authority than the entire heavily armed, globe-spanning British Empire. He is demonstrating the ultimate triumph of "good"—using pure, self-sacrificing non-violence to successfully break the most powerful colonial machine in history.
