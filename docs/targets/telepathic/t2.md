## RV Target Sheet: 3819-5024

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 3819-5024
*   **Historical Identity:** J. Robert Oppenheimer
*   **Time Period:** July 16, 1945, early morning (exactly 05:29 AM).
*   **Event / Timeline:** The "Trinity" test — the moment of detonation of the first-ever plutonium bomb.
*   **Location:** S-10000 control bunker (Compañía Hill), Jornada del Muerto desert, New Mexico, USA. Approximately 16 kilometers (10 miles) from Ground Zero.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A man of average height (approx. 178 cm / 5'10"), but extremely, almost pathologically emaciated (weight approx. 52 kg / 115 lbs). His posture is tense, hunched from stress and cold, giving the impression of physical fragility.
*   **Face:** Ascetic, elongated, with sharp cheekbones. Very deep, dark circles under his eyes indicate a chronic lack of sleep and physical exhaustion.
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Dark, closely cropped hair, prematurely graying at the temples. Very light (blue) eyes with an expression of feverish, almost painful concentration.
*   **Attire:** He wears a civilian suit that seems a bit too loose for his emaciated frame, and an unbuttoned shirt. The entire outfit is rumpled after long hours spent in high tension. He almost constantly holds a burning cigarette in his fingers.

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** Extreme, vibrating nervous tension, balancing on the edge of a physical breakdown. His analytical mind compulsively processes countdown data, while a deeper, philosophical part of his consciousness grapples with the unimaginable ontological weight of the impending phenomenon.
*   **Emotions:** A mix of paralyzing fear (dread of complete failure or atmospheric catastrophe) and deep, existential terror. There is a palpable sense of crossing an irreversible boundary.
*   **Behavior:** Just before the detonation, he barely breathes. To control severe physical trembling, he clings tightly to a wooden post supporting the bunker's roof. He chain-smokes. When the flash occurs, his face relaxes for a fraction of a second in an expression of immense relief, only to freeze moments later in absolute, dark reflection (the moment of recalling verses from the *Bhagavad Gita*).

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** A cramped, half-buried shelter built of thick wooden beams, earth, and reinforced concrete.
*   **Interior:** Crammed with military and scientific equipment. Control panels, thick communication cables, switches. The room is stuffy, crowded with other men in military and civilian clothing.
*   **Visual Stimuli:** Inside, there is twilight; outside, the deep, pre-dawn darkness of the desert. The climax is an absolute, initially silent flash of light. This light is brighter than the sun, unnaturally sterile, retina-burning (observed through thick, dark welder's glass), transitioning into blinding white, and then into a gigantic, purple-orange fireball.
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** Before the detonation, only the steady, mechanical voice counting down the seconds through a speaker and the hum of the apparatus can be heard. At the moment of the flash, there is complete, eerie silence that lasts for several dozen seconds. This is followed by a crushing, deep roar, resembling an elongated thunderclap, which brutally shakes the entire surroundings.
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** A dense, sharp smell of tobacco smoke, sweat, and damp earth (following a night storm). Right after the shockwave passes, the air becomes saturated with a metallic, suffocating scent of ionized air (ozone).
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** Initially, the damp chill of the desert morning. At the moment of the explosion (with a delay equal to the shockwave), a blast of an unnatural, dry wave of heat penetrating through the cracks. A physical, brutal trembling of the ground and walls beneath their feet.

### Social Standing
*   The man is the civilian scientific director of the most secret, expensive, and consequential military project in human history. He commands absolute intellectual authority among the greatest minds of that era, while simultaneously existing within a strict hierarchy of subordination to military generals. He bears the pressure of the entire state apparatus. In this fraction of a second, he becomes a sort of "high priest" of a new, terrifying technological era—the man who has just handed the world the tool for its own self-destruction.
