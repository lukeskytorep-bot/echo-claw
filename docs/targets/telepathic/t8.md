## RV Target Sheet: 9472-1158

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 9472-1158
*   **Historical Identity:** J.P. (John Pierpont) Morgan
*   **Time Period:** November 2 to the early morning hours of November 3, 1907.
*   **Event / Timeline:** The climax of the Panic of 1907. The moment he locks the city's top trust company presidents inside his private library, refusing to unlock the heavy bronze doors until they sign an agreement to pool $25 million to save the collapsing United States economy.
*   **Location:** The East Room of the Pierpont Morgan Library, 36th Street and Madison Avenue, New York City, USA.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A massive, physically intimidating man in his 70s. He has a broad, heavy-set frame that projects immense, immovable physical authority. 
*   **Face:** His most defining and striking feature is his nose—it is severely deformed, bulbous, and purple due to a chronic skin condition (rhinophyma). He is fiercely self-conscious about it, yet uses his terrifying visage to dominate others. 
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Thinning white hair and a thick white mustache. His eyes are fiercely intense, famously described by contemporaries as looking "like the headlights of an express train bearing down on you."
*   **Attire:** Impeccably dressed in the height of Edwardian plutocratic fashion. A heavy, custom-tailored dark three-piece suit made of the finest wool, a stiff white collar, a silk cravat, and a heavy gold watch chain draped across his expansive waistcoat. 

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** Supreme, unshakeable arrogance and absolute confidence. While the men in the next room are in a state of frantic panic over the collapsing stock market, his mind is coldly analytical and utterly composed. 
*   **Emotions:** He feels a mixture of irritation and disdain for the "weakness" of the other bankers. He possesses a god-like conviction that it is his personal responsibility—and right—to single-handedly stabilize the nation's finances. He experiences no fear of bankruptcy, only a desire for order.
*   **Behavior:** Deliberately ignoring the chaos, he sits alone at a small table in front of a roaring fireplace, silently playing a game of Solitaire. He smokes one massive Havana cigar after another. He does not yell; when he does speak, it is in a low, gruff, absolute command. He simply waits for the exhausted men in the adjacent room to break to his will.

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** A newly constructed, breathtakingly opulent private library built like a Renaissance palace. The walls are made of solid Tennessee pink marble, and the massive front doors are solid bronze. 
*   **Interior:** The epitome of concentrated wealth. Floor-to-ceiling tiers of bronze bookcases containing priceless, centuries-old illuminated manuscripts, Gutenberg Bibles, and original works of antiquity. The room is decorated with rare Renaissance tapestries and a massive antique fireplace. 
*   **Visual Stimuli:** The room is warmly lit by the flickering orange glow of the roaring fire and soft, green-glass reading lamps. The light catches the gold leaf stamped on the spines of thousands of rare books. The air is visibly thick and hazy with blue cigar smoke.
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** The sharp, rhythmic *snap* of playing cards being placed on a polished wooden table. The heavy, slow ticking of an antique grandfather clock. Through the heavy wooden doors, the muffled, desperate, and exhausted arguments of dozens of trapped millionaires pleading with each other in the adjacent room. 
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** An incredibly rich, suffocating blend of extreme luxury. The dominant smell is thick, premium Cuban cigar smoke (he smoked dozens a day). Beneath that is the scent of burning hardwood from the fireplace, expensive cologne, polished mahogany, and the distinct, dusty vanilla scent of ancient paper, vellum, and leather bindings.
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** The crisp, smooth texture of high-quality playing cards in his thick fingers. The plush, heavy red velvet of the armchair he sits in. The room is uncomfortably warm, stiflingly heated by the large fire and the sheer number of nervous, sweating men locked inside the heavily insulated building. 

### Social Standing
*   The man represents the absolute apex of global capitalist power. He is not merely a wealthy industrialist; he is the unofficial central bank of the United States. He wields more immediate, raw financial power than the US President. The men he has locked inside his house are multi-millionaires themselves—titans of industry and banking—yet in his presence, they are reduced to cowering, exhausted subordinates. He is the ultimate "master of the universe," dictating the fate of the American economy from his private armchair.
