## RV Target Sheet: 6610-3847

### Identification and Timeframe
*   **Target Number:** 6610-3847
*   **Historical Identity:** Joseph Stalin (Iosif Vissarionovich Dzhugashvili)
*   **Time Period:** Late autumn 1937, roughly 3:00 AM.
*   **Event / Timeline:** The absolute height of the Great Purge (The Great Terror). Sitting alone at his desk in the middle of the night, systematically reviewing and signing execution lists containing thousands of names of former comrades, military officers, and citizens.
*   **Location:** His private office in the Little Kremlin Palace, Moscow, USSR.

### Physical Appearance
*   **Stature:** A short man (approx. 165 cm / 5'5") with a somewhat stocky build. His posture is slightly stiff and uneven due to a withered, partially paralyzed left arm (from a childhood carriage accident), which he tends to keep tucked near his body.
*   **Face:** His complexion is deeply scarred and pockmarked from childhood smallpox. He has a heavy, thick, dark mustache that hides his mouth. 
*   **Hair and Eyes:** Thick, bristly, dark hair brushed straight back, showing traces of gray. His eyes are a distinct yellowish-brown, often described by contemporaries as cold, watchful, and predatory ("like a tiger's").
*   **Attire:** Deceptively modest and utilitarian. He wears a plain, khaki military-style tunic (a "French" tunic) entirely devoid of medals, epaulettes, or rank insignia. Baggy trousers tucked into soft, black, heel-less leather boots. His clothing projects the image of a simple, hardworking servant of the state.

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
*   **State of Mind:** A state of profound, calculating paranoia and total megalomania, masked by absolute calmness. He perceives conspiracies, betrayals, and threats in every shadow. His mind operates like a cold, ruthless machine, viewing the elimination of thousands of people as a necessary "pruning" or "cleaning" of the state apparatus. 
*   **Emotions:** He feels no anger, rage, or guilt. There is only a chilling, bureaucratic detachment to mass murder. He experiences a grim, quiet satisfaction in outsmarting and destroying his perceived enemies before they can strike him.
*   **Behavior:** He works nocturnal hours, forcing the entire Soviet government and military apparatus to stay awake in terror, waiting for his calls. He paces the large room with a slow, heavy, rolling gait. His soft boots make almost no sound on the carpets. He frequently stops at his desk, takes a thick red or blue colored pencil, and casually signs "I. St." or simply writes "Shoot all" at the bottom of lists containing hundreds of names. 

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
*   **Physical Structure:** A large, rectangular, highly secure, and soundproofed office deep within the walls of the Kremlin fortress. The walls are covered in heavy wood paneling.
*   **Interior:** Austere and intimidating. A massive, heavy oak desk dominates the space. Nearby is a long conference table covered in green baize cloth. The walls feature large maps of the Soviet Union and portraits of Marx and Lenin. There are high stacks of paper folders (the execution protocols) piled on the desk.
*   **Visual Stimuli:** The room is plunged in deep, heavy shadows. The primary source of light is a desk lamp with a thick, green glass shade, which casts a harsh pool of yellowish light strictly onto the papers and his hands, leaving the corners of the massive room in darkness. 
*   **Acoustic Stimuli:** Oppressive, deadened silence. The thick carpets and heavy doors absorb all outside noise. The only sounds are the ticking of a grandfather clock, the soft rustle of paper, the scratching of his thick wax pencil against the documents, and his almost completely silent footsteps. 
*   **Olfactory Stimuli:** The room is heavy with the distinct, strong smell of "Herzegovina Flor" tobacco. He habitually breaks apart these specific, high-quality Russian cigarettes to pack their loose, dark tobacco into his wooden pipe. Beneath the smoke is the smell of old paper, leather upholstery, and floor wax.
*   **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** The smooth, polished, warm wood of his smoking pipe in his right hand. The physical stiffness and slight ache in his withered left arm. The warmth radiating from the green desk lamp. The coarse, thick texture of the bureaucratic paper lists under his fingertips. The sensation of absolute, isolated security within the thick stone walls.

### Social Standing
*   The man is the unquestioned, totalitarian dictator of the largest country on Earth. He is arguably the most feared individual on the planet at this moment. He possesses god-like, unchecked power over the life and death of millions. His mere name induces physical trembling and cold sweats in his highest-ranking generals and ministers. He has stripped away all traditional power structures and replaced them with a state apparatus designed entirely around absolute terror and submissive devotion to him personally.
