## RV Target Sheet: 7492-8103

### Identification and Timeframe
* **Target Number:** 7492-8103
* **Historical Identity:** Nikola Tesla
* **Time Period:** Summer and autumn nights of 1899.
* **Event / Timeline:** The moments of conducting critical experiments with wireless power transmission and standing waves.
* **Location:** Experimental station, Colorado Springs plateau, USA.

### Physical Appearance
* **Stature:** A very tall man (approx. 188 cm / 6'2") and extremely thin (weight approx. 64 kg / 141 lbs). His posture is erect, almost unnaturally rigid and statue-like.
* **Face:** Elongated, with sharp, angular features and sunken cheeks.
* **Hair and Eyes:** Very dark, thick hair, smoothly combed straight back. Light-colored eyes (gray-blue), characterized by an incredibly intense, hypnotic, and piercing gaze.
* **Attire:** Utterly unsuited for the harsh laboratory environment, yet consistent with his nature. He wears an impeccably tailored, dark woolen suit, a white shirt with a stiff, high collar, and a tie. He frequently wears light-colored suede or leather gloves. His clothing is always sterilely clean and entirely wrinkle-free.

### Psychological Profile and Behavior
* **State of Mind:** Deep, almost non-human concentration. The mind operates in a highly organized, visionary manner. In the background, there is strong tension stemming from obsessive-compulsive tendencies (compulsive counting of steps, fixation on the number 3).
* **Emotions:** Radical emotional isolation. Dominated by a cold intellect, a sense of absolute certainty regarding his visions, and a complete lack of fear toward the lethal forces he manipulates. Cold and detached in relation to his surroundings.
* **Behavior:** Movements are sparse, highly precise, deliberate, and distinguished. He demands absolute silence and unconditional execution of orders from his few assistants. He suffers from mysophobia (fear of germs)—categorically avoiding touching other people or unclean surfaces. During the activation of the apparatus, he freezes completely still, fully absorbed in analyzing the phenomena.

### Surroundings and Sensory Stimuli (Environment)
* **Physical Structure:** A crude, barn-like wooden building situated on an open plain. It features an opening roof, through which a copper mast several dozen meters high pokes vertically upward, topped with a sphere.
* **Interior:** Controlled technical chaos. The space is filled with a tangle of thick cables, massive capacitors, and a gigantic coil (resonant transformer) measuring over a dozen meters in diameter. Devoid of any residential or decorative elements—pure functionality.
* **Visual Stimuli:** The interior is shrouded in twilight, which is violently torn apart by powerful, blinding arc discharges. The light has a sharp, pulsating, violet-blue color.
* **Acoustic Stimuli:** A deafening, rhythmic roar and crackle of artificial lightning, resembling artillery fire inside a closed room. The sound is so immense that it generates physical vibrations traveling through the ground.
* **Olfactory Stimuli:** An extremely dense, pungent smell of ozone (a hyper-intensified scent of the air immediately following a lightning strike).
* **Tactile/Kinesthetic Stimuli:** The air feels "dense" with electrostatic charges. A distinct tingling sensation on the skin, hair standing on end, and a palpable feeling of being inside a powerful magnetic field.

### Social Standing
* The man holds the status of a global genius, yet at the same time, he is perceived as a controversial loner and eccentric. He operates outside the traditional academic world. He lives exclusively for his work, has no family, and does not accumulate private wealth (funneling all investor funds directly into experiments). Among the local, rural community of Colorado Springs, he evokes unease and fear, sometimes referred to as a dangerous wizard disrupting the peace with "artificial storms" and knocking out the town's power generators.
