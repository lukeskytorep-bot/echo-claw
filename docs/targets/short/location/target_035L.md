# Target 0035

**Target coordinates:**  435e tr54  
**Date of the target:** July 2020  

**Target:** High-altitude alpine terrain and the Rüfikopf cable car mountain station (Rüfikopf-Seilbahn Bergstation - 47.1994° N, 10.1650° E (Schafalpweg, Lech, Austria)).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a high-altitude alpine environment and its associated tourist infrastructure. The scene is set on a rugged, exposed mountain ridge during the summer season. The environment is characterized by extensively fractured, light-colored rocky terrain interspersed with patches of green alpine grass and residual snow. The focal point of human infrastructure is a cable car terminal and adjacent lodge situated on the highest local peak. 

---

### Part 1: Geological and Natural Environment

*   **The Terrain:** The ground is predominantly composed of uneven, heavily weathered light-grey rock (characteristic of karst/limestone landscapes). The surface is highly irregular, requiring hikers to navigate over fragmented stone steps and boulders.
*   **Vegetation and Climate:** Despite the July date, the climate at this altitude is harsh. Vegetation is limited to low-lying, bright green alpine grass and small yellow wildflowers growing in the crevices between the rocks. Shallow depressions in the rocky landscape still hold broad, flat patches of melting white snow.
*   **The Macro View:** The surrounding background consists of a vast, sweeping panorama of distant, jagged mountain ranges under a partly cloudy blue sky. Several of the distant peaks also retain visible snowfields.

### Part 2: The Infrastructure (Rüfikopf Station)

*   **The Station Complex:** Situated on the prominent peak to the right of the hiking trail is the Rüfikopf cable car upper station. It consists of two main connected structures:
    *   A mechanical terminal with a large, angular steel pylon supporting the thick cables that stretch down into the valley.
    *   An adjoining multi-story building, painted in a faded green color, featuring a steep, slanted roof and multiple rectangular windows, functioning as a mountain lodge/restaurant.
*   **Slope Reinforcements:** On the steep, grassy incline immediately below the cable car station, multiple rows of wooden avalanche barriers (snow fences) are installed to prevent snow movement during the winter.
*   **Trail Details:** A solitary, simple wooden bench is placed on a flat rocky outcrop, offering a resting point for tourists.

### Part 3: Human Activity

*   **The Hikers:** Small groups of tourists are visible traversing the rocky landscape. They are dressed in standard summer hiking gear (shorts, t-shirts, backpacks) and are using the marked trails to navigate the uneven elevation changes.
