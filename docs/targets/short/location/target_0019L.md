# Target: Postojna Cave (Infrastructure and Tourist Route - 45°46′57.6″N 14°12′13.2″E)

The presented target is Postojna Cave (Slovenia), one of the longest cave systems in the country (over 24 km) and a world-renowned tourist attraction. The target covers both the external infrastructure (entrance zone) and the internal underground route.

## External Infrastructure and Entrance Zone:

* **Main Building:** Directly at the main entrance, there is a large, historic building ("Jamska restavracija"). It is characterized by a bright, sandy-yellow facade, a row of arcades on the ground floor, and decorative balconies (the flag of Slovenia is hanging there). A wide square, paved with light and dark slabs, extends in front of the building.
* **Cave Entrance:** The main entrance is a massive, natural opening (grotto) in a vertical rock wall, overgrown with dense vegetation at the top.
* **Tourist Zone:** The entrance square is bustling with activity. Numerous large, bright umbrellas with the park's logo are set up there, under which tourists gather waiting to enter (marked with posts and ropes). Around them, there are information points (an "i" sign) and souvenir stands.

## Internal Infrastructure and Underground Route:

The route available to tourists is about 5 km long, most of which is covered by underground transport. The temperature inside is constant and amounts to about 10°C (50°F).

* **Underground Train:** A key element of the infrastructure is the electric cave train. Tourists travel in simple, open, metal cars with blue seats, moving along narrow tracks inside the cave. This infrastructure historically dates back to 1872 (initially, the cars were pushed by guides).
* **Pedestrian Paths:** Pedestrians walk on concreted, anti-slip paths, secured with solid metal railings in a black or rusty color. The route is highly modernized and illuminated with artificial, warm light, which allows thousands of people to move safely (the "Concert Hall" can accommodate up to 10,000 people).

## Geological Formations (Speleothems):

The interior of the cave abounds in spectacular, illuminated karst formations:
* **"Brilliant":** The symbol of the cave, a massive, shiny white stalagmite with a characteristic, bipartite shape.
* **Tube (or Spaghetti) Hall:** A hall from the roof of which hang thousands of tiny, tubular stalactites resembling spaghetti.
* Also visible are massive stalagnates (columns connecting the floor with the ceiling), formations called "curtains" (draperies), and complex dripstone cascades (like the formations in the picture captioned "A formation of stalagmites...").
