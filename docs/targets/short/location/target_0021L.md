# Target: Adršpašsko-teplické skály (Rock City, Czech Republic)

The presented target is the Adršpašsko-teplické skály National Nature Reserve, located in the Czech Republic, near the towns of Adršpach and Teplice nad Metují. It is the largest rock city in the Czech Republic and a unique sandstone massif, forming part of the Table Mountains. This location is a highly organized, ticketed tourist attraction of significant natural and geological importance.

## Tourist Infrastructure and Trails:

Basic sightseeing information indicates that exploration takes place along strictly designated trails; venturing off the paths is strictly prohibited (except for climbers with a special permit).
* **Main trail (Adršpach):** Marked in green, it is 3.5 km long (approx. 3 hours of walking). It leads through the most famous rock formations. The route is highly modernized – equipped with stairs, wooden and metal footbridges, ladders, and protective barriers, making it accessible even to inexperienced hikers.
* **Other trails:** The "Pískovna" route (blue, 1.5 km, marked as a barrier-free trail), the trail connecting through Vlčí rokle (Wolf Gorge, yellow, 3.5 km), and the trail around the Teplice Rocks (blue, 6 km).
* **Facilities:** At the foot of the massif (e.g., near Pískovna and the Adršpach entrance), there are main entrances, information centers, and restrooms (which are not available along the trail itself). Paid public transportation is accessible nearby.

## Geology and Main Attractions:

The massif was shaped by water, ice, and wind, which carved monumental towers out of the sandstone (some nearly 100 meters high, such as the "Lovers" at 81.4 m) and deep canyons (only 50 cm wide in the narrowest section).

The main attractions and key points of this place include (with Czech formation names):
1. Džbán (The Pitcher)
2. Homole cukru (Sugar Cone)
4. Gotická branka (Gothic Gate) - a historical gate built into a narrow crevice between vertical rocks.
8. Eliščina věž (Elizabeth's Tower)
13. Velký vodopád (Great Waterfall) - a cascade dropping 16 meters down, surrounded by mossy sandstone walls.
14. Skalní jezírko (Rock Lake) - located on the upper level, offering boat rides.
16. Milenci (The Lovers)
21. Pískovna (Sandpit Lake) - a flooded former sandpit with turquoise water, visible near the entrance.

## Environment and Ecosystem:

* **Nature:** The massif is a unique biotope (hence its National Nature Reserve status). The rocks are densely covered with vegetation – mosses and ferns dominate in the lower, shaded areas (forming intensely green carpets on the rocks), while coniferous trees grow higher up (often taking root in narrow crevices and on top of the towers).
* **Teplické skály (southern part):** These have a slightly different character – they are wilder, mainly forming long, faulted walls and canyons, unlike the branched labyrinth of Adršpach.

## Activity:

* Hiking along designated, often narrow trails and steep stairs.
* Boat cruises on the upper Rock Lake.
* Relaxing by the Pískovna Lake.
* High-class sandstone climbing (rock cities are among the most famous terrains of this type in the world).
