# Target: Szklarki Waterfall (Karkonosze Mountains)

The presented target is a natural geological formation and its adjacent tourist zone. The main point is the Szklarki Waterfall, located in the Karkonosze mountain range, along with its immediately accompanying visitor infrastructure.

## Environment Dynamics and Mechanics:

* **Water Cascade:** The central element of the target is the dynamic, gravitational fall of water masses. The river current breaks over a rocky threshold and cascades down from a height of 13 meters. The stream narrows towards the bottom, creating a frothy, white torrent that strikes the rocky riverbed with great force, generating splashes and forcing a continuous, swift flow of water between the boulders.
* **Human Activity:** The space around the natural formation is adapted for organized tourist traffic. People move on foot along paved paths and gather for observation on designated viewing platforms, which are safely separated from the riverbed by massive metal railings.

## Environment and Infrastructure:

* **Natural Surroundings:** The waterfall is deeply embedded in a steep, shaded river valley. This terrain is characterized by a dense, lush mixed forest tightly covering the rocky slopes. The surroundings of the riverbed are moist, strewn with huge, rounded erratic boulders, many of which are covered with moss.
* **Artificial Objects:** In the immediate vicinity of the waterfall, integrated into the steep forest slope, is the "Kochanówka" mountain shelter. It is a brick, multi-story building with a light-colored facade, serving as the main dining and resting point in this location. Around it is smaller infrastructure supporting tourist traffic (benches, small stalls, stairs).
