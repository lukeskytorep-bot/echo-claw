# Target 0037

**Target coordinates:**  6546 7677  
**Date of the target:** July 2014  

**Target:** High-altitude alpine hiking trail, the "Dom Śląski" mountain shelter, and the approach to the Śnieżka peak on the Poland-Czech Republic border - Approx. 50.737° N, 15.729° E (Dom Śląski / Base of Śnieżka, Karkonosze Mountains) 

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a heavily trafficked, high-altitude pedestrian transit node located directly on an international border. The scene is set in an exposed alpine tundra environment during the summer season. The focal points of the area are a massive, brightly colored mountain shelter serving as a logistical base, a wide stone-paved ascent route, and a steady stream of human subjects (tourists) navigating the terrain toward the dominant, conical peak rising in the immediate background.

---

### Part 1: Architecture and Infrastructure

*   **The Mountain Shelter (Dom Śląski):** Situated on a flat saddle is a large, multi-story rectangular building functioning as a mountain shelter and restaurant. The structure features a highly visible, bright yellow facade with multiple rows of small, dark rectangular windows. It is topped with a dark, pitched roof containing dormer windows. Several service vehicles (including a dark blue van) are parked near its side entrance.
*   **The Main Pathway:** The primary transit route is a wide, continuous road paved entirely with rough, uneven, light-grey granite cobblestones. This rigid, artificial surface provides a stark contrast to the surrounding natural terrain. 
*   **Barriers and Signage:** Sections of the stone pathway are bordered by simple, utilitarian barriers consisting of vertical metal posts and horizontal tubular metal rails. These are installed to keep pedestrian traffic strictly on the paved surface and protect the fragile alpine ecosystem. Wooden information boards with small green roofs are positioned near the shelter.

### Part 2: Topography and Natural Environment

*   **The Vegetation:** The surrounding terrain is classic alpine tundra. It consists of low-lying, dense patches of dark green mountain dwarf pine (kosodrzewina) interspersed with open areas of short, dry mountain grass and exposed, weathered rocks. There are no tall trees due to the high altitude.
*   **The Dominant Peak (Śnieżka):** Rising massively in the immediate background to the east is Śnieżka, the highest peak in the range. Its slopes are steep, rocky, and mostly barren of vegetation near the top. The distinct, saucer-shaped silhouette of the summit meteorological observatory can be seen at the very top of the peak against the partly cloudy sky.
*   **The Borderline:** As indicated by the geographic mapping data, the international border separating Poland and the Czech Republic runs directly through this environment, closely following the ridge and the main hiking trail.

### Part 3: Kinetic/Human Activity

*   **Pedestrian Traffic:** A continuous, highly active stream of hikers is moving along the cobblestone path in both directions. The subjects are dressed in varied summer hiking apparel, including shorts, windbreakers, and backpacks, many utilizing trekking poles to navigate the incline. The area immediately around the yellow shelter serves as a congested gathering and resting point.
