# Target: Desert and agricultural lands near the Awjila oasis (Libya)

The presented target is a desert area located on the outskirts of the town/oasis of Awjila in Libya. It is a place where the natural landscape of the Sahara Desert meets organized agricultural activity.

## Natural Environment:

* **Terrain:** The ground is completely flat, sandy, and dry. The color of the sand is a very light beige/white, which is characteristic of desert areas with strong sunlight. There are no hills, dunes, or rocks visible around.
* **Vegetation:** The landscape is heavily modified by plantings. Dense rows of trees are visible. On one side, dark green, coniferous, or very small-leaved trees grow, forming a compact, dark windbreak. Deeper in, on the light sand, smaller, light green trees or large shrubs grow at regular intervals. This contrasts with the dry surroundings.

## Infrastructure and Activity:

* **Fences:** A simple, primitive fence laid directly on the sand is visible. It consists of dried branches, reeds, or palm leaves tightly arranged along a line, creating a barrier or windbreak against blowing sand. 
* **Traces of activity:** Rows of small trees and irrigation or protective lines (dark strips on the sand and rows of dried vegetation) clearly indicate organized agricultural activity and attempts to develop the desert area, probably using irrigation. Among the trees stands a single, white pole or pillar.
* **Macro-environment:** An aerial perspective confirms that the point is located on the southwestern edge of the dense, bright buildings of the oasis, which borders the endless, flat expanses of the desert to the west, intersected only by straight, linear traces of roads heading inland.
