# Target: Junell Street (Houston, Texas)

The presented target is residential infrastructure in the form of an American suburban neighborhood, located on Junell Street in Houston, Texas.

## Environment and Infrastructure:

* **Spatial Layout:** The area is a flat terrain covered with a grid of streets, featuring dense single-family housing hidden among trees. The street itself is asphalt, completely lacking curbs and pedestrian sidewalks. On both sides of the road, there are wide, unpaved drainage ditches overgrown with grass.
* **Architecture:** The buildings consist of single-story houses with simple shapes and multi-pitched roofs. The house facades vary, built with light brick featuring red accents or finished with light horizontal siding. Individual properties are separated from each other and from the road by solid wooden fences or simple metal chain-link fences.

## Natural Environment and Human Traces:

* **Vegetation:** The terrain is characterized by the presence of many old, very tall, and spreading deciduous trees. A significant portion of the tree canopies is leafless, reflecting the season visible in the photos. On one of the open front lawns lies a massive, uprooted tree trunk along with its torn root system.
* **Traces of Activity:** The space is actively used by local residents. Various vehicles are parked on the unpaved, gravel driveways in front of the buildings, including passenger cars, minivans, and pickup trucks. Small elements of municipal infrastructure are located in the landscape right next to the road, such as black mailboxes on wooden posts and a blue fire hydrant.
