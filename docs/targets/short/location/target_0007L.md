# Target: Cosca Village and Ancient Ruins (Near Volcán Ollagüe)

The presented target is an isolated settlement and archaeological site situated within a deep desert canyon. It is located at Cosca, near the Volcán Ollagüe, featuring a stark contrast between arid geology and a localized green oasis.

## Environment and Topography:

* **Desert Canyon:** The macro-environment is a vast, extremely arid, and rocky landscape. A deep canyon with steep, sheer cliff faces cuts through the plateau. The canyon walls and surrounding highlands are barren, reddish-brown, and completely devoid of natural vegetation.
* **Valley Oasis:** The canyon floor harbors a narrow, linear strip of vibrant green vegetation. This area includes cultivated agricultural fields, trees, and shrubs, sustained by a localized water source flowing through the valley bottom.

## Infrastructure and Human Activity:

* **Historical Ruins:** A prominent, steep-sided rocky ridge runs parallel to the valley floor. The crest of this ridge is completely covered with extensive, ancient stone ruins. These structures consist of densely packed, labyrinthine stone walls forming numerous small, rectangular, roofless enclosures.
* **Modern Settlement:** Adjacent to the green zone in the valley floor is a contemporary settlement consisting of simple, mostly flat-roofed buildings. The settlement is connected by a winding dirt road (labeled as Ruta a Cosca).
* **Recreational Facility:** A highly contrasting modern artificial structure is present near the settlement: a rectangular sports field (football pitch) with bright green artificial turf and white boundary lines.
