# Target: Wooden Bridge on the Forest Trail (Świeradów-Zdrój)

The presented target is a point of tourist infrastructure consisting of a wooden footbridge located in a densely forested mountainous area. The object is situated in the town of Świeradów-Zdrój (Poland), directly on the hiking trail connecting the "Czeszka" Tourist Complex with the viewpoint located at the site of the old ski jump.

## Environment and Topography:

* **Terrain Characteristics:** The macro-environment is a rolling mountainous landscape covered with a dense, mature mixed forest, dominated by tall spruce trees. The area exhibits high humidity levels.
* **Water Feature:** A shallow but dynamic mountain stream flows beneath the wooden bridge. Its bed is natural and irregular, filled with numerous large glacial boulders, many of which are covered with a thick layer of moist green moss.
* **Vegetation and Surface:** The forest floor and the immediate surroundings of the trail are covered with lush, vibrant green undergrowth, including grasses, mosses, and ferns. The trail itself is unpaved, consisting of a dirt and gravel mix with visible irregularities, small stones, and exposed soil.

## Architecture and Infrastructure:

The massive protective railings are built from raw, unbarked logs, giving the structure a rustic character. Upon closer inspection, the side planks of the deck show signs of slight physical deterioration and decay, though the central portion of the structure appears functionally intact.
* **Safety Information:** Directly before the entrance to the bridge, a white warning sign with a prominent red triangle is mounted on a thin post. This sign explicitly alerts visitors that the bridge is not in optimal condition and requires caution when crossing.
* **Recreational Area:** A short distance from the bridge, an organized rest area is located on a grassy clearing. It consists of two identical wooden shelters with gabled roofs, featuring integrated picnic tables and benches.
