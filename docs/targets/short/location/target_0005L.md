# Target: Old Windmill and Water Tank (Bulloo Downs, Australia)

The presented target is an isolated agricultural and technical infrastructure located in a dry, desert environment in Australia, in a place called Bulloo Downs. The main element is the "Old Southern Cross Windmill" and an adjacent dried-up water tank.

## Environment Dynamics and Mechanics:

* **Wind Mechanism:** The central point is a tall, steel lattice tower topped with a multi-blade rotor (wind turbine) and a directional tail. This device, although currently static in the photos, is used to harness wind energy to mechanically drive a pump and extract groundwater to the surface.
* **Resource Accumulation (Tank):** At the base of the windmill is a large, open, cylindrical tank with thick walls. It is currently completely empty, and its bottom is covered with a cracked, dried layer of earth, indicating a long-term lack of water and harsh climatic conditions. Right next to the tank, there is also a long trough (waterer) for animals.

## Environment and Infrastructure:

* **Desert Landscape:** The surroundings consist of a vast, flat plain covered with characteristic red soil. Vegetation is extremely sparse, limited to rare, low, and dried shrubs and isolated tufts of grass. The horizon is expansive, dominated by a mostly cloudless blue sky with light, high-altitude clouds.
* **Traces of Human Activity:** Despite the immense isolation, there are clear signs of human intervention. From an aerial perspective, the terrain is intersected by numerous crossing sandy tracks and tire marks. Around the infrastructure, a simple agricultural wire fence is stretched on wooden and metal posts. In the distance, two white off-road vehicles are parked.
