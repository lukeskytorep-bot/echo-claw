# Target: Technical Monument - Steam Locomotive Tp4-217 (Świeradów-Zdrój)

The presented target is a historic steam locomotive serving as a static technical monument, located near an active railway station in the town of Świeradów-Zdrój.

## Main Object (Steam Locomotive Tp4-217):
* **Visual Characteristics:** A massive, black steam locomotive with a raw, steel structure, bearing visible signs of aging and corrosion. The machine is positioned on an isolated, short section of track laid on gravel ballast, separated from the road by a low wire fence.
* **History and Specifications:** According to the on-site documentation, it is a Tp4-217 model manufactured in 1918 by Borsig Lokomotiv Werke in Berlin. It features a 0-4-0 axle arrangement, a total length over buffers of 18.29 m, an empty weight of 63.61 t, and an indicated power of 926 kW (max speed 55 km/h). After decades of service in state railways and the Polish coal industry, the machine became a monument and was exhibited at this location in September 2019.

## Station Surroundings and Infrastructure:
* **Culture Station (Stacja Kultury):** Behind the locomotive lies the area of an active railway station (MCKiPG "Stacja Kultury"). The main station building is a restored, multi-body structure with a light-colored facade and a red, multi-pitched tiled roof.
* **Technological Contrast:** Parked on a platform in the distance is a modern passenger railbus (in white, yellow, and black livery), creating a strong chronological contrast with the rusting locomotive. A few individuals can be seen moving around the station area.
* **Characteristic Elements:** At the entrance to the station area sits a massive, vertical boulder with the carved name "Stacja Kultury Świeradów" and a bas-relief of a locomotive. Nearby, distinct blue-and-white directional signs are mounted on the fence, indicating the entrance to "Platform 1, Track 1".

## Natural Environment and Topography:
* **Terrain Characteristics:** While the immediate area of the station has been artificially leveled by humans, the surrounding terrain is clearly sloping. Mountains are visible in the distance, confirming that the entire complex is situated within a mountainous region.
* **Water Feature and Bridge:** In the immediate vicinity of the monument, intersecting the terrain, flows a mountain river, introducing a continuous rushing sound to the environment. Next to the station entrance is a road bridge spanning the river, distinguished by its brick structural elements.
* **Vegetation:** The entire area is characterized by lush nature. The surroundings of the station, the riverbed, and the monument itself are dominated by tall, mature deciduous trees in an intense, spring shade of green.
