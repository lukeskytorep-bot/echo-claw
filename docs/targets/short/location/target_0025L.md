# Target: Krzyżna Góra in the Falcon Mountains (Poland)

The presented target is the summit of Krzyżna Góra (Cross Mountain, 654 m above sea level), which is one of the two main peaks of the Falcon Mountains (Góry Sokole), located in the northwestern part of the Rudawy Janowickie range. This location combines rich natural values, raw granite rock formations, and historical tourist infrastructure.

## Natural Environment and Landscape:

* **Vegetation:** In the lower sections, the trail leads through a dense, lush deciduous and mixed forest. Large, old trees are visible (some trunks bear carved initials and tourist graffiti), along with undergrowth rich in ferns and mosses. 
* **Geology:** The mountain is built of Karkonosze granite. Approaching the summit, massive, vertical walls and boulders emerge from the forest, giving the place a rugged character. There are also numerous other rock formations and crags scattered throughout the surrounding area.
* **Panorama:** The observation platform at the summit offers a vast, impressive view of the surrounding valleys, meadows, built-up areas, and distant mountain ranges.

## Tourist Infrastructure and Trail:

* **Approach:** The route evolves from a dirt forest path to a steep climb. The final stage of reaching the summit requires ascending steep, irregular stairs carved directly into the granite rock.
* **Safety features:** A characteristic element of the trail's infrastructure are tubular metal barriers and handrails painted green. They accompany tourists on the steep sections of the stairs and secure the observation platform at the top.
* **Educational amenities:** An information board with a described panorama is mounted at the viewpoint, making it easier to identify the visible peaks and towns.

## Main Attractions and Activity:

* **The Rocky Summit and Cast Iron Cross:** The summit itself is a distinct, exposed granite rock outcrop, visually surrounded by the tops of the trees below. Its central and most recognizable feature is a massive, seven-meter cast-iron cross (dating back to 1830) mounted directly on the rock. It towers over the observation platform, serving as a historical monument founded by Princess Maria Anna Amalie.
* **Castle Ruins:** Located near the foot of the rocky summit are the historical, though currently almost invisible and heavily overgrown, stone ruins of a medieval castle (Sokolec Castle).
* **Human Activity:** The site is actively visited by hikers and tourists traversing the stairs and viewing platforms. Furthermore, because of the abundance of vertical rock walls in the vicinity, the area is highly popular among people who actively practice rock climbing.
