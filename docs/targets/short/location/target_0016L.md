# Target: Dirt Road in the Equatorial Forest (Ndolou, Gabon)

The presented target is a remote, unpaved transportation route cutting through a dense equatorial forest. According to the geolocalization data, this area is located in the Ndolou department of Gabon (Central Africa).

## Natural Environment:

* **Vegetation:** The landscape is completely dominated by a lush, multi-layered jungle (rainforest). Massive, very tall trees with spreading crowns as well as intensely green, dense undergrowth and vines are visible, reaching the very edge of the road.
* **Waterway:** Right next to the road, in a depression of the terrain, flows a small, shallow river or stream with turbid, brownish water. Fallen, dead tree trunks lie in the riverbed and along its banks.
* **Ground and Climate:** The road is sandy-clay, with a bright, yellow-brown coloration that contrasts sharply with the green of the forest. The sky is completely overcast, which is typical for the humid equatorial climate prevailing in this region.

## Infrastructure and Activity:

* **Road and Logging:** The wide dirt road shows signs of regular use and leveling with heavy machinery (distinct ruts and wide tire tracks). On the roadsides, cleared parts of the slope and horizontally arranged, cut trunks of massive trees can be seen. This suggests that the route is actively used by the logging industry for timber transport.
* **Vehicle and People:** On a widened section of the roadside, a white off-road vehicle is parked with its hood raised. A person is standing directly next to it, which may indicate a mechanical failure or a planned technical stop in the field.
* **Signage:** Single, makeshift stakes are driven along the edge of the road (some with a white element attached), which most likely serve as temporary landmarks, warning points, or surveying markers.
* **Macro-environment:** Broad geographic context confirms that this is an extremely isolated area, surrounded on all sides by an unbroken belt of dense forest. The target is located near small, remote settlements such as Sika or Bindéli, west of the R23 regional road.
