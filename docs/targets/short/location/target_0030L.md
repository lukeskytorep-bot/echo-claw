# Target 0030 - Location

**Target coordinates:** r83e ue83
**Elevation:** 681 m a.s.l. (m.n.p.m.)  
**Date of the target:** May 2018  

**Target:** Krucze Skały (Raven Rock - Lubawka, Poland) - High-altitude natural viewpoint and nature reserve.

**Description:**
The target is a high-altitude natural viewpoint situated on a rocky peak known as Krucze Skały (Raven Rock), located within the Kruczy Kamień nature reserve. At an elevation of 681 meters, it offers a commanding panoramic view over a wide, vibrant valley. The environment is characterized by steep, rugged cliffs that drop off dramatically into densely forested slopes. The surrounding area features varied topography, pointing towards agricultural lands and distant settlements nestled between rolling hills.

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Terrain and Viewpoint
* **The Summit:** The observation area features exposed, rough bedrock, loose dirt, and sparse patches of wild grass. 
* **The Trail:** A distinct, well-worn dirt trail leads across the ridge, providing access to the edge of the cliff.
* **The Drop-off:** The edge of the viewpoint presents a severe, nearly vertical drop into the valley below, showcasing the raw geological formations of the area.

#### Vegetation and Natural Landscape
* **Summit Flora:** The immediate rocky outcrop is dotted with scattered birch trees and larches, maintaining a relatively open canopy that allows for unobstructed views.
* **The Forests:** The steep slopes immediately below and surrounding the peak are covered in a dense, continuous blanket of green coniferous and deciduous forest.
* **The Valley:** Looking outward, the landscape opens up into a vast, flat patchwork of vibrant green meadows and brown plowed fields.

#### Human Infrastructure and Surroundings
* **Safety Railings:** A rudimentary wooden safety railing is installed along the most dangerous edges of the cliff to protect visitors from the steep drop. 
* **Metal Pole:** Further back on the trail, a tall, green metal pole structure stands isolated on the grassy ridge.
* **Nearby Landmarks:** Satellite mapping of the immediate vicinity indicates the presence of nearby geographical and structural landmarks, including "Dolina Miłości" (Valley of Love) to the south and an old ski jumping hill designated as "Skocznia narciarska HS-94 Krucza Skała" to the northeast.
