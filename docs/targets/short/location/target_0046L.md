# Target 0046

**Target ID:** 992J BO41  
**Date of the target:** Present day (Established 1945)  

**Target:** Jodrell Bank Observatory, Cheshire, England.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a major radio astronomy observatory located in a rural setting. The site is dominated by a colossal steerable parabolic dish supported by a massive steel lattice framework. The surrounding grounds feature modern and mid-century administrative buildings, a visitor center, and an arboretum.

---

### Part 1: The Main Telescope (Lovell Telescope)

*   **The Primary Structure:** The central structural feature is the Lovell Telescope, a massive steerable radio telescope with a parabolic dish diameter of 250 ft (76 m).
*   **The Framework:** The massive dish is supported by a colossal, intricate white steel lattice framework. 
*   **Mobility:** The entire structure rests on a circular outer railway track, allowing the immense telescope to rotate and steer.
*   **Scale:** Its dimensions make it the third largest steerable radio telescope in the world.

### Part 2: The Observatory Infrastructure

*   **Main Administrative Complex:** Adjacent to the main telescope is a complex of flat-roofed, multi-winged administrative and research buildings constructed of brick and white paneling.
*   **Secondary Telescopes:** A smaller, secondary white radio telescope dish is visibly mounted on the roof of one of these research buildings. The site hosts three other active telescopes, including the Mark II, a 42 ft (13 m) telescope, and a 7 m diameter radio telescope.
*   **Visitor Facilities:** The facility includes a modern visitor center (the Jodrell Bank Discovery Centre, opened in 2011), which features a glass-walled cafe, exhibition pavilions, and a space dome.

### Part 3: The Environment and Terrain

*   **The Landscape:** The observatory is situated in a flat, rural landscape in Cheshire, England, surrounded by agricultural fields and woodland.
*   **The Grounds:** The immediate grounds are heavily landscaped, featuring expansive green lawns, paved red pedestrian pathways, and a 35-acre (140,000 m2) arboretum containing diverse tree collections.
*   **Transport Links:** A railway line (the Crewe to Manchester Line) runs directly past the perimeter of the site.
