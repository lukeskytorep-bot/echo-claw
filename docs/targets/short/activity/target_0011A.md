# Target: "Flying Kung Fu" Stunt Simulation

The presented activity is an organized, commercial tourist attraction (taking place at Wild Duck Lake Resort), allowing participants to experience first-hand stunt sequences known from action movies and Wuxia cinema.

## Mechanics and Activity Flow:

* **Wirework:** Participants are secured with special harnesses hidden under their clothing and attached to thin wires. This computer-controlled system lifts them into the air and regulates their trajectory and speed while moving over the lake.
* **Staging and Props:** Fighters wear stylized, loose-fitting costumes with wide cuts, which enhances the visual impression of fluidity during jumps. They hold props in the form of long swords with decorative tassels, performing mid-air duels.
* **Evolutions:** The attraction allows for hanging in mid-air (even upside down), spectacular lunges toward an opponent, crossing blades in flight, and "skimming" across the water's surface, where feet brush the water at high speed, creating splashes.

## Movement Dynamics and Environment:

The scene is based on a distinct physical and visual contrast.

* **Water Environment:** Water serves as the background and boundary. It reacts only to contact with the rushing participants, throwing up splashes in the form of droplets and creating wavy reflections.
* **Participants:** Suspending them in space requires physical effort and constant balance. The movement visible in the air is determined by intention and focus, showing purposeful tension throughout the body at the moment of a strike, followed by a rapid change of positioning.

In the background, an artificial structure is also visible, consisting of moored docks lined with tires and water equipment, which serves as the technical and organizational base for this entertainment area.
