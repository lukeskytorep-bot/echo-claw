# Target: The World's Biggest Barbecue (Guinness World Record), Montevideo, Uruguay

The presented target is a massive outdoor culinary event organized in a park in Montevideo, Uruguay, during which the then-Guinness World Record for the world's largest barbecue was broken. This event, organized by the National Meats Institute (INAC), aimed to promote Uruguayan beef on the international stage.

## Scale and Infrastructure:

* **Spatial layout:** An aerial view shows a gigantic, organized structure laid out on the plan of a massive circle. The grills are formed into radiating rows converging towards the center (resembling a spiked flower or a sun with rays). The terrain itself is distinctly flat and open, predominantly covered in green, indicating a large grassy area. Along the perimeter of the event, various structures and buildings (such as a prominent building with a red roof) are visible, enclosing the park grounds.
* **Logistics and equipment:** The total length of the connected grills was nearly 1.5 kilometers. 1,500 metal stands were used to set them up, with the army assisting in the construction. For practical reasons, 6 tons of charcoal were used for roasting, which had to be lit and supervised by firefighters to ensure the flames did not get out of hand.
* **Amount of food:** 12,000 kilograms (12 tons) of beef were simultaneously placed on the grills, roasted in very large, massive chunks visible in the close-up shots.

## Activity and Participants:

* **Cooks:** Around 1,250 "grillmeisters" worked directly by the fire. They were uniformly dressed in white t-shirts/aprons and classic, tall chef's hats. They used long metal tools (tongs and pitchforks) to turn the heavy slabs of meat. Due to the extreme amounts of stinging smoke, some cooks covered their faces with bandanas.
* **Crowd:** Beyond the inner working circle, the grill structure was surrounded by a dense ring of approximately 20,000 spectators (including schoolchildren, officials, and tourists). The area was secured by barriers, from behind which the crowd could safely observe the event.
* **Atmosphere:** The entire area inside the circle was shrouded in very thick, billowing clouds of smoke from the charcoal and roasting meat. The event had an incredibly dynamic, bustling, and massive character.
