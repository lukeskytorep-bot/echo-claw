# Target 0034

**Target ID:** 445X EV94  
**Date of the target:** 1994  

**Target:** An untethered American astronaut performing an extravehicular activity (EVA) (Low Earth Orbit).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a human subject operating independently in the vacuum of space. The scene captures a single astronaut floating freely in microgravity, entirely untethered from any spacecraft. The subject is positioned against the massive backdrop of the Earth, which fills the entire background of the camera's perspective.

---

### Part 1: The Extravehicular Mobility Unit (Spacesuit)

*   **The Suit:** The human figure is completely encased in a bulky, pressurized, multi-layered white spacesuit designed to sustain life in a vacuum. The suit features articulated joints and distinct red identification stripes located around the lower thighs/knees.
*   **The Helmet:** The subject's head is enclosed in a rigid helmet featuring a large, highly reflective mirrored gold visor. This visor completely conceals the astronaut's face and reflects the harsh, unfiltered sunlight of the orbital environment.
*   **The Interface:** The astronaut's hands, enclosed in thick, heavily insulated white gloves, are positioned in front of the chest. They are actively manipulating a mechanical control module extending from the front of the suit via a structural tether.

### Part 2: The Propulsion System (Backpack)

*   **The Hardware:** Attached securely to the lower back and base of the primary life-support backpack is a rigid, rectangular metallic unit.
*   **Function:** This geometric, block-like structure is a specialized maneuvering propulsion system (such as the SAFER unit). It houses pressurized gas and small thrusters, allowing the astronaut to manually control their pitch, roll, and yaw, and to navigate independently without a physical tether to the nearby space shuttle.
*   **Kinetic State:** The astronaut is in a state of free-fall, oriented horizontally relative to the camera, with the legs slightly bent at the knees.

### Part 3: The Orbital Environment (Background)

*   **The Backdrop:** The entire visual background beneath the floating astronaut is filled by the surface of the Earth from a high-altitude orbital vantage point.
*   **Atmospheric Features:** The planet's surface is heavily obscured by a vast, continuous layer of highly textured, puffy white clouds (a stratocumulus field).
*   **The Ocean:** Through the various breaks and geometric gaps in the dense white cloud cover, patches of deep blue ocean water are clearly visible.
