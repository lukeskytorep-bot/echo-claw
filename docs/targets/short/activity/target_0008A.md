# Target: Guinness World Record Firewalk Attempt

The presented target is an organized, extreme outdoor event involving an official attempt to break the Guinness World Record for the longest barefoot walk over hot coals (firewalk) covering a distance of 250 meters. The action takes place on a grass sports field surrounded by an athletics track, encompassing both a daytime preparation phase and a nighttime culmination phase.

## Site Preparation and Infrastructure:

* **Wood Piles:** In the initial, daytime phase of the event, numerous piles of chopped wood were arranged at regular intervals on the grass. The area was secured and cordoned off with tape, with sports infrastructure and technical facilities visible in the background.
* **Track Creation:** After dark, the wood piles were ignited. Once they burned down, creating glowing coals, the technical team (wearing bright orange high-visibility vests) began forming the path. Using steel wheelbarrows and shovels, the crew precisely scattered and leveled the hot embers, creating a very long, continuous track of coals directly on the grass.
* **Safety:** Red signaling flags were placed around the track, and the entire area was monitored by a professional safety team (including members of "Firebird Firewalks"). 

## Event Dynamics and Main Act:

* **Support and Ritual:** Before stepping onto the path of fire, a personal ritual took place. The main participant received a red citrine quartz from her team, charged with intentions of support, which accompanied her throughout the walk. The event was heavily supported by the local community and numerous sponsors.
* **The Walk:** At the culminating moment during the night, the main participant, dressed in a striking red outfit, stepped barefoot onto the glowing track. She held burning torches in both of her raised hands, providing a strong visual and symbolic element.
* **Atmosphere:** The walk took place under the glare of strong floodlights illuminating the field. The participant was accompanied by a dense crowd of spectators and supporters, gathered just behind the designated safety tape line. The crowd actively observed, filmed, and cheered the attempt, creating an atmosphere of high energy and deep focus.
