# Target: SpaceX Falcon 9 Manned Space Mission Launch (Crew-2)

The presented activity is a historic, successful launch of the Falcon 9 launch vehicle, manufactured by SpaceX, as part of a manned space mission carried out in cooperation with NASA. The event took place on April 23, 2021, at 11:49 AM, taking off from the launch pad at Cape Canaveral in Florida, United States.

## Mechanics and Event Dynamics:

* **Ignition and Ascent Phase:** The rocket lifts off from the ground, propelled by the massive thrust of its engines. The scene is dominated by an extremely bright, almost blinding pillar of fire and light (thermal and luminous energy) emitted from the nozzles, which illuminates the dark background of the sky.
* **Launch Pad Environment:** Massive, dense clouds of smoke and water vapor gather at the base next to the launch tower. These are the result of burning massive amounts of rocket fuel and the operation of the water sound suppression system protecting the platform during ignition.
* **Vertical Movement Vector:** The activity is characterized by a continuous, controlled, and extremely dynamic movement vector directed vertically upwards.

## Mission Target and Crew:

At the top of the speeding rocket is a spacecraft – the Crew Dragon capsule named "Endeavour". It constitutes an artificial, highly technologically advanced, and hermetic life-support environment. Inside, there is a four-person, international crew heading to the International Space Station (ISS). The team includes:

* **Shane Kimbrough** – Mission Commander (NASA)
* **Megan McArthur** – Pilot (NASA)
* **Akihiko Hoshide** – Mission Specialist (Japan)
* **Thomas Pesquet** – Mission Specialist representing the European Space Agency (ESA) from France
