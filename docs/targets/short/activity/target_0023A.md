# Target: Traditional Easter Carbide Cannon Shooting (Podkarpacie Region, Poland)

The presented target showcases the highly vibrant regional tradition of carbide cannon shooting cultivated during the Easter period by local firefighters from the Volunteer Fire Department (OSP). The activity takes place outdoors on a flat, grassy residential yard, utilizing custom-welded artillery pieces to detonate acetylene gas, generating a powerful, ritualistic explosion.

## Cannon Construction and Preparation Sequence

* **The Custom Cannon:** Instead of utilizing traditional plastic or tin containers, experienced crews construct a dedicated cannon consisting of a thick, heavy black metal pipe. This barrel is dimensionally sized just a bit larger than a human arm, resting directly on the ground and securely welded onto a stable, weighted base runner designed to counteract massive recoil forces.
* **Chemical Reaction:** Calcium carbide chunks are dropped inside the metal pipe along with water, triggering an immediate chemical reaction that rapidly generates highly explosive acetylene gas within the chamber. A thin, faint cloud of white smoke can be seen escaping from the barrel during this initial staging phase, signaling the active gas release.
* **Pressure Sealing:** To maximize internal gas pressure before detonation, a firefighter wide-stances and bends over the front flange, using a hammer to dynamically strike and tightly drive home the front cap or lid to block the outlet. 

## Ignition and Explosion Mechanics

* **The Touchhole Ignition:** At the rear of the heavy metal barrel is a small touchhole designed for ignition. A firefighter stands ready, extending a very long metal rod or wooden pole serving as a remote igniter to introduce an open flame to the slot while maintaining a safe physical distance.
* **Pre-Detonation and Flash:** As the flame is brought precisely to the rear slot, a yellow-orange glow builds inside the gap, culminating in a pinpoint flash and a sharp, localized flame at the touchhole. 
* **The Blast:** The flame reaches the main gas chamber, triggering a violent ignition of the accumulated acetylene gas. A bright, prominent sheet of fire bursts from the rear of the cannon. The explosion generates an incredibly loud, deafening roar, forcing the igniting firefighter to instinctively raise his left hand to tightly cover his ear and shield his hearing from the blast. Automated text overlays displaying fragments like "bar" and "bar jest jest Zabierz" appear temporarily on screen during these sequences.
* **Post-Blast Dynamics:** The immense force of the explosion forcefully expels the front sealing cap, leaving a lingering trail of thick, characteristic white carbide smoke around the device. Small, controlled spots of residual fire continue to burn on the grass patch right next to the metal runner immediately after the blast.
* **Tactical Retreat:** Immediately following the touchhole ignition, the operator executes a quick, springy step away, retreating rapidly to the right and turning his head entirely away from the blast zone as the camera pans dynamically to track his movement.

## Personnel and Staging Environment

* **Firefighter Attire:** The operators carry out the ritual in full ceremonial black Volunteer Fire Department dress uniforms, featuring gala jackets adorned with bright honorary function cords draped over the shoulder.
* **The Landscape:** The setting is a flat, open grassy residential yard featuring a white single-family house standing in the slight distance to the left. The yard is bordered by a property fence line and a silhouette of distant trees along the horizon under a daylight sky.
