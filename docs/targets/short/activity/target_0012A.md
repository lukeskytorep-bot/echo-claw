# Target: Intentional Demolition of a Beached Sperm Whale Carcass (Florence, Oregon, USA)  

The presented target details the historical event of an intentional beach cleaning operation where state officials attempted to dispose of a massive, beach-stranded whale carcass using heavy explosives. The resulting detonation led to unexpected blast dynamics, widespread dispersion of organic debris, and significant collateral property damage.

## Carcass Dimensions and Initial Staging

* **The Subject Mass:** The target involves a deceased 45-foot-long sperm whale carcass that washed ashore on a sandy coastal beach.
* **Physical Scale:** The whale carcass is estimated to weigh approximately 8 short tons (16,000 lbs).
* **Initial Staging:** The dark, elongated mammalian body rests directly on the wet sand near the ocean shoreline. Small groups of onlookers, beachgoers, and state officials stand on the dry sand dunes in the background to observe the pre-blast operations.
* **Atmospheric Context:** The dynamic unfolds on a wide, misty coastline under an overcast sky with ocean waves breaking heavily in the distance.

## Personnel and Explosive Preparation

* **Project Leadership:** The operation is managed by the Oregon Highway Division under the on-site engineering supervision of George Thornton.
* **Media Presence:** A television news crew, including a reporter and a cameraman, is actively on-scene recording interviews and logistics prior to detonation. The supervising engineer is interviewed on the beach wearing a silver hard hat, a striped blue turtleneck, and a bright orange jacket.
* **Explosive Payload:** Workers wearing yellow construction hard hats transport and place a massive charge consisting of twenty cases, totaling one-half short ton (1,000 lbs), of dynamite beneath the carcass.

## Detonation and Blast Dynamics

* **The Explosion:** Upon detonation, a colossal, vertical geyser of sand, smoke, and pulverized organic matter erupts violently into the air.
* **Debris Dispersion:** The force of the half-ton blast functions poorly as a disintegration mechanism; instead, it funnels a massive hole into the sand and channels a destructive arc of debris backward toward the spectator zones.
* **Lingering Aerosols:** The blast completely blankets the immediate coastal horizon in a dense cloud of beige smoke and falling particulate matter.

## Aftermath and Collateral Damage

* **Widespread Impact:** Large chunks of blubber and whale flesh are launched up to 800 feet away from the epicenter.
* **Property Damage:** Falling organic debris lands heavily in distant parking lots and near beachfront buildings. A dark passenger sedan parked near the dunes suffers catastrophic structural damage, with its metal roof crushed and caved entirely inward by a falling chunk of blubber.
* **Debris Clearance:** Scavenger birds are frightened away by the deafening sound of the blast and fail to consume the remains. Heavy machinery, specifically a yellow tracked bulldozer, must be deployed on the sand to manually bury and scrape away the large remaining portions of the carcass left scattered across the beach.
