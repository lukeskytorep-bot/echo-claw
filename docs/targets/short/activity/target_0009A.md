# Target: Timed Hot Dog Eating Duel (Chestnut vs. Kobayashi)

The presented target is a formalized sports competition and entertainment event (known as "competitive eating"), involving the timed consumption of hot dogs. This event, broadcast live on Netflix under the title "Chestnut vs. Kobayashi: Unfinished Beef," marked a direct clash between two historic rivals after a 15-year hiatus. HyperX Arena, Las Vegas, USA

## Participants and Rules:

* **Joey Chestnut:** The US representative, a 40-year-old competitor weighing over 100 kg. During the event, he wore a short-sleeved, two-tone shirt (white bottom, dark top) with the words "UNFINISHED BEEF".
* **Takeru Kobayashi:** The Japanese representative, a 46-year-old competitor weighing 71 kg. He wore a fitted, dark sleeveless shirt.
* **Competition Rules:** The goal was to eat as many hot dogs along with the bun as possible within a strictly defined 10-minute time limit. There was a strict ban on separating the meat from the bun, and dunking the food in water was not allowed, which significantly hindered the consumption process.

## Course of Events and Sports Result:

The duel was characterized by immense dynamics and physical effort from the competitors. Joey Chestnut won the competition by eating 83 hot dogs within the designated time. With this result, he not only defeated Kobayashi (who ate 66 portions) but also broke his own historic world record from 2021 (which was 76 hot dogs). The winner received a financial prize of $100,000.

## Event Infrastructure and Environment:

* **Stage and Competition Zone:** The struggles took place inside a large entertainment arena (HyperX Arena). The focal point was a long, curved table piled high with trays of hot dogs and grey event-branded beverage cups.
* **Competitor Separation:** Directly between Chestnut and Kobayashi, a tall, transparent divider (made of plexiglass or glass) was mounted on the tabletop, ensuring physical separation of the rivals during the intense act of eating.
* **Background and Audience:** Behind the competitors, large LED screens displayed their faces and event-related graphics. The entire scene was brightly illuminated by dynamic, warm spotlights. Right in front of the stage, a dense, enthusiastically reacting crowd gathered, constantly observing and cheering the participants. The area around the competitors was heavily serviced by television broadcasting equipment and camera operators.
