# Target 0014 - Activity  
Date of target: 27 August 2011  
Target coordinates: 7864 6453    

**Target:** Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series at Swallow's Nest (Lastivchyne hnizdo), near Yalta, Crimea.

---

**Description:**
The target area is defined by a massive, steep terrain formation—a towering, rugged rock cliff dominating the vertical space right over the sea. Perched precariously on the absolute summit of this peak is the Swallow's Nest, a highly compact human structure built of stone, wood, and stucco, serving as an observation point and a historical symbol of the region. About halfway down the sheer face of this mountain, a temporary horizontal track of matter has been anchored into the rock: a diving platform. 

The primary activity involves human athletes performing daredevil, twisting descents from this immense height into the landing zone below. The receiving environment is the salty Black Sea, which presents a cool, synchronized rhythm without its own intention, acting purely as a deep, liquid boundary that reflects the light and impact. Surrounding this calm aquatic baseline is a massive concentration of biological activity. Over 15,000 spectators gather on the rocky shores and in a sprawling armada of boats, manifesting as warm, point-like, and imperfect impulses with a clear direction of focus toward the cliff. The event culminates in highly energetic emotional tension, followed by rapid relief and wet celebrations among the athletes at the water level.

---

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Terrain and Natural Landscape
*   **The Cliff (Cape of Ai-Todor):** A steep, dominant, and irregular mountain formation of raw, fractured rock rising vertically from the Black Sea. It serves as the unyielding foundation for both the historical architecture and the extreme sports infrastructure.
*   **The Water:** The landing zone is a fluid substance, measured at over 5 meters (around 16 feet) deep. It maintains a cool, synchronized rhythm, disturbed only by the high-velocity impact of the divers and the wakes of numerous spectator vessels.

#### Architecture and Human Structures
*   **The Swallow's Nest Castle:** A prominent human structure built in the late 19th century. It is exceptionally compact, measuring only 20 meters (66 feet) long by 10 meters (33 feet) wide. It features a foyer, guest room (decorated with wooden panels), a stairway to the tower, and two bedrooms. An observation deck rings the exterior, filled with onlookers viewing the distant Yalta shoreline.
*   **The Diving Platform:** An engineered, artificial projection securely bolted into the cliff face exactly 26 meters (85 feet 3 inches) above the water surface. 

#### Activity and Human Pulse
*   **The Jump:** Athletes (including the series winner, 27-year-old Briton Gary Hunt) perform highly calculated, twisting leaps. This represents a rhythmic but imperfect biological effort, characterized by intense concentration, a brief period of extreme physical stress during the freefall, and quick relief upon safely breaking the water's surface.
*   **The Crowd:** A dynamic field of over 15,000 fans encircles the base of the cliff. They are positioned on adjacent rocks, the beach, and across dozens of yachts, dinghies, and speedboats, creating a dense ring of warm, biological energy. Underwater photographers are also submerged in the area, hunting for specific visual angles.
*   **Celebration:** Post-competition activity includes athletes celebrating on floating pontoon platforms, joyously spraying champagne and splashing the salty water over one another.

#### Climate and Weather Conditions (27 August 2011)
*   **Temperatures:** The environment presents a mild, warm summer climate. The atmospheric air temperature sits at 23°C (73.4°F), while the water temperature is only slightly cooler at a comfortable 21°C (69.8°F).
*   **Atmosphere:** The sky is partially filled with scattered, bright white clouds, allowing for excellent visibility and striking natural lighting against the pale stone of the castle and the dark rock of the cliff.
