# Target 0032

**Target ID:** 592P SC10  
**Date of the target:** 2010  

**Target:** The UK Pavilion (colloquially known as the "Seed Cathedral") at the Shanghai World Expo 2010.

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a unique sculptural pavilion built for the 2010 World Expo in Shanghai. The structure is a rounded cuboid standing six stories high, pierced by tens of thousands of long, clear acrylic rods, giving it a fuzzy or dandelion-like appearance. The architecture utilizes the rods to act like fiber optics, transmitting light and showcasing hundreds of thousands of plant seeds suspended within the structure.

---

### Part 1: The Exterior Architecture

*   The central building is a rounded cuboid standing 20 metres (66 ft) tall.
*   The structural core consists of a 1-metre-thick steel and timber composite frame.
*   Piercing this frame are 60,000 thin, 7.5-metre (25 ft) long acrylic fibre-optic filaments held in precision-drilled aluminium sleeves.
*   These rods protrude extensively outward, swaying in the wind and drawing in external daylight to the interior.

### Part 2: The Interior and the Seeds

*   The interior forms a curving space illuminated during the day entirely by the natural light channeled through the 60,000 acrylic rods.
*   At the interior tip of each 20-millimetre (0.79 in) square-sectioned filament, one or more plant seeds are permanently encased in the clear acrylic.
*   In total, the structure houses 250,000 seeds sourced from the Kunming Institute of Botany.
*   At night, artificial light sources within the filaments illuminate the interior and project light outward, making the entire structure appear to glow.

### Part 3: The Surrounding Site

*   The pavilion sits in the centre of a 6,000-square-metre (65,000 sq ft) United Kingdom exhibition site.
*   The surrounding grounds are landscaped with artificial turf.
*   The pavilion's main entrances, exits, and additional spaces for environmental exhibits are located on an underground level beneath the landscaped area.
