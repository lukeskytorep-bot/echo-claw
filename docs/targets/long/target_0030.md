# Target 0030

**Target ID:** 774T RR69  
**Date of the target:** May 10, 1869 (approx. 12:47 p.m. local time)  

**Target:** The "Golden Spike" ceremony marking the completion of the First Transcontinental Railroad at Promontory Summit, Utah, USA (41° 37′ 4″ N, 112° 33′ 5″ W).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a major industrial and infrastructural milestone. The scene captures two heavy steam locomotives meeting face-to-face on a newly completed railway track in a remote, arid landscape. The central focus is the physical and mechanical connection of two massive infrastructure networks, surrounded by a large, stationary crowd of workers and officials.

---

### Part 1: The Mechanical Vehicles (Steam Locomotives)

*   **The Machines:** Two 19th-century steam locomotives stand stationary on the same single track, facing each other with only a few feet of separation. They are constructed primarily of heavy iron, brass, and wood.
*   **Locomotive "Jupiter" (Left):** The engine on the western side features a wide, flared "balloon" smokestack designed to catch wood embers, a prominent cowcatcher at the front, and a large box headlight.
*   **Locomotive "No. 119" (Right):** The engine on the eastern side has a straight, cylindrical smokestack (as it burned coal) and a similarly prominent front headlight and cowcatcher.
*   **Kinetic State:** While stationary, both machines are active, venting plumes of white steam and smoke from their stacks and cylinders into the air above the track.

### Part 2: The Infrastructure and Terrain

*   **The Railway:** A single, continuous line of parallel iron rails rests on heavy wooden cross-ties, which are embedded in a bed of crushed rock and graded dirt. 
*   **The Landscape:** The setting is a dry, barren, and flat high-desert plateau. The ground is composed of dusty brown earth and scattered, low-lying scrub brush, entirely devoid of large trees or urban structures.
*   **Telegraph Lines:** Running parallel to the iron tracks is a line of simple, vertical wooden telegraph poles connected by a single suspended wire, representing an accompanying communication network.

### Part 3: The Crowd and the Connection

*   **The Gathering:** A dense crowd of hundreds of men (railroad workers, engineers, military personnel, and officials) is clustered tightly around the front of the two trains. Several individuals have climbed up and are standing directly on the front boilers of the locomotives.
*   **The Focal Point:** At the exact center point on the ground, in the narrow gap between the front pilots of the two facing engines, the final structural connection is being made.
*   **The Final Spike:** A polished wooden tie (made of laurel wood) is laid across the track bed. A ceremonial spike made of solid gold is placed into a pre-drilled hole in the wood, serving as the final physical pin connecting the eastern and western iron rails together.
