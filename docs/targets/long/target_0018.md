# Target 0018

**Target coordinates:** Jfg5 yt65  
**Date of the target:** Circa 2000-2009  

**Target:** A Kyrgyz Sheep Ranch and Seasonal Pastures. (Kyrgyzstan - General location)

**Description:**
The target is a traditional sheep ranch located in a relatively flat valley within a high-altitude, deeply mountainous region of Kyrgyzstan. The environment features sprawling valleys, rugged peaks, and seasonal pastures utilized for livestock grazing. The primary human habitation consists of base camps featuring both permanent rustic structures and traditional portable "Yurts". The overall activity centers entirely around "transhumance"—the seasonal, cyclical migration of massive sheep flocks between low-altitude winter camps and high-altitude summer pastures. The human presence is deeply integrated with the remote landscape, relying heavily on the livestock for food, heating, and lighting.

---

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Natural Landscape and Geography
*   **Mountain Ranges:** The region is dominated by massive mountain ranges that transition from green slopes to barren, snow-dusted brown peaks. High-altitude, jagged, snow-capped peaks form the permanent backdrop of the region.
*   **Valleys and Plains:** The base camps are situated in relatively flat, narrow river valleys at elevations between 1000 and 2000 meters. The terrain varies from dry, dusty dirt around the pens to golden-brown grassy plains and vibrant green upland pastures.

#### Architecture and Human Habitation
*   **The Yurt:** A prominent feature is the traditional Kyrgyz Yurt, a round-topped structure made of matted wool stretched over a wooden frame. The interior features a dirt floor covered by rugs and contains portable furniture.
*   **Base Camp Infrastructure:** Alongside the yurts, there are rudimentary permanent structures, including a small wooden shack and a stone-paved patio area.
*   **Off-Grid Living:** These settlements lack electricity and piped water. Heating and cooking are powered by burning collected wood or dried sheep manure, while lighting is provided by lamps burning sheep fat.

#### Livestock and Transhumance (Migration Activity)
*   **The Sheep Pens:** At the base camp, a large, irregular enclosure made of rough wooden fencing holds a dense flock of hundreds of sheep and goats.
*   **The Flocks:** Massive flocks consisting of mixed-color sheep and goats are seen grazing and moving across the terrain.
*   **The Migration (Transhumance):** The herds are in constant seasonal motion. Shepherds, sometimes riding on horseback or walking on foot, guide vast flocks across flat plains, up grassy ridges, and along paved winding mountain roads.
*   **Cyclical Movement:** The activity follows a strict cycle: wintering in lower valleys, a slow upward migration in spring/early summer, grazing in high mountain pastures in July and August, and a rapid descent back to base camps in autumn.

#### The Human Element
*   **The Herders and Families:** The local population consists of Kyrgyz families, including men, women, and young children, who live and work together in these remote conditions. They rely heavily on their livestock, including sheep, cows, and horses, for meat and milk.
