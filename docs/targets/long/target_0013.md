# Target 0013

**Target coordinates:** 4838 8382  
**Date of the target:** May 2026

**Target:** Deepspot (Mszczonów, Poland) - Indoor deep diving pool and training facility.

**Description:**
The target is a massive, highly engineered indoor aquatic facility entirely dedicated to scuba diving and freediving. It consists of a modern, heavy-duty industrial building housing a colossal, artificially maintained body of water. The internal environment is a sharply geometric, multi-tiered basin that transitions from a wide, shallow recreational area near the surface into a dramatic, extremely deep vertical cylindrical shaft plunging down to a maximum depth of over 45 meters. The water is perfectly clear, heavily filtered, and artificially heated (32-34°C), creating a highly controlled, synthetic maritime environment entirely isolated from the natural world outside. The space is populated by human divers suspended at various depths, interacting with several artificial underwater structures and props.

---

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Aquatic Environment (The Pool)
* **Water Volume and Quality:** The basin holds approximately 8,000 to 9,100 cubic meters of pristine, highly transparent water. The extreme clarity allows for visibility from the surface directly down into the deepest sections. The water appears vibrant turquoise-blue due to the lighting and tile colors.
* **Geometry and Surfaces:** The walls and floors of the main upper basins are strictly angular and lined with a grid of pale blue, white, and grey ceramic tiles. Large, bold black lines and numbers are painted vertically on the walls to clearly indicate the depth (e.g., -5m, -10m, -15m... down to -45m).
* **The Deep Shaft:** At the bottom of the main terraced basin, the floor opens into a perfectly circular, concrete-lined vertical tube (the "blue hole") that drops straight down to the facility's maximum depth.

#### Internal Structures and Artificial Anomalies
* **The Viewing Tunnel:** A transparent, semi-circular acrylic tunnel is suspended horizontally just below the water's surface across the main basin. This structure allows dry spectators to walk directly through the submerged environment, creating a distinct boundary between the dry, atmospheric space and the aquatic zone.
* **The Ruins / Cave System:** Built into one side of the facility is an enclosed, dark, artificial cave system. The walls here are textured to look like ancient stone blocks, featuring a massive, intricately sculpted faux-stone face (resembling historical Southeast Asian/Khmer temple architecture) gazing into the water.
* **Submerged Props:** Various artificial objects are deliberately placed on different ledges to serve as training targets or points of interest. These include a sunken white motorboat resting on a flat mid-level ledge, and a metallic, spherical diving bell (underwater habitat) suspended by a thick cable in the water column.

#### Human Activity
* **Divers:** The primary biological presence consists of divers (both scuba divers with tanks and freedivers with long fins) moving rhythmically and deliberately through the water. Because the water is exceptionally warm, many are diving in standard swimwear or light rash guards rather than thick neoprene wetsuits. 

#### Exterior and Enclosure
* **The Building:** The external structure housing the pool is a large, modern, rectangular facility. The facade features dark grey/black cladding with vertical light-wood accents and a large white "DEEPSPOT" logo. The roof over the main pool area includes a large, multi-paned glass skylight structure, allowing natural sunlight to penetrate the upper layers of the water.
