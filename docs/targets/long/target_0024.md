# Target 0024

**Target coordinates:**  4637 8728  
**Date of the target:** Thursday, July 16, 2026  

**Target:** The controlled explosive demolition of the Commercial Street Bridge - Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA (Parkway East)

**Description:**
The target is a highly kinetic, massive engineering and demolition event taking place in a lush, wooded valley. The scene revolves around the intentional destruction of an aging concrete arch bridge to make way for a fully constructed replacement. The focal point of the event is the exact moment of detonation, where the rigid concrete structure is instantly pulverized, creating a massive, expanding atmospheric anomaly of dust and debris before settling into a pile of rubble.

---

### Part 1: Pre-Demolition State and Infrastructure

*   **The Old Structure:** The original Commercial Street Bridge was a large infrastructure project featuring prominent, classic concrete arches supporting the roadway. Prior to the explosion, the bridge had already been partially dismantled and stripped down by construction crews.
*   **The Replacement:** Directly parallel to the old bridge, a brand-new, flat concrete bridge structure had already been fully built and rested on temporary structural supports.
*   **The Environment:** The bridges span a wide, deep valley characterized by dense, vibrant green foliage and trees.

### Part 2: The Kinetic Event (The Implosion)

*   **The Detonation:** At approximately 8 a.m. on Thursday, crews detonated a synchronized series of explosives rigged to the old bridge's remaining supports. 
*   **Visual Anomaly:** The blast instantly shattered the concrete pillars and arches. A colossal, chaotic cloud of thick grey and light-brown dust violently erupted from the structure, expanding rapidly outward and entirely obscuring the old bridge from view. 
*   **Debris:** Large fragments of concrete and debris could be seen flying outward from the leading edge of the massive dust cloud as the structure collapsed downward into the valley.

### Part 3: The Aftermath and Human Element

*   **The Resolution:** Once the massive dust cloud dissipated, the old arch bridge was completely gone, reduced to a large pile of grey rubble at the bottom of the valley. The newly constructed bridge remained completely intact, standing quietly next to the destruction.
*   **The Engineering Plan:** Following the demolition, the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT) will execute a massive lateral slide, moving the new bridge into the exact footprint of the old one at a speed of 19 feet per hour. This operation is recorded as the largest lateral slide project performed in the state.
*   **The Observers:** The dramatic event drew the attention of local onlookers, some of whom had gathered on nearby vantage points in 90-degree heat with cameras and tripods to witness the historic demolition.
