# Target 0017

**Target coordinates:** Cooper's Hill, Gloucestershire, England  
**Date of the target:** 2012  

**Target:** Gloucester Cheese Rolling 2012 - Extreme downhill race and traditional cultural event.

**Description:**
The target is a highly kinetic, chaotic biological event taking place on an extremely steep, almost vertical natural terrain formation. The environment is a massive grassy slope heavily bordered by a dense, green forest barrier. The primary activity involves human participants launching themselves down the treacherous incline in aggressive pursuit of a fast-moving, solid disc of cheese. The event is characterized by a severe loss of physical control, high-velocity tumbling, and a massive concentration of spectators tightly lining the edges of the active zone. The contrast between the rigid, unyielding gravity of the hill and the fragile, completely uncontrolled falling bodies creates a scene of brutal dynamics and high physical danger.

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Terrain and Natural Landscape
* **Cooper's Hill (The Drop):** A legendary, extremely steep natural incline covered by a wide, green grassy band. The surface is highly uneven, creating an incredibly dangerous, frictionless track for any rapid descent.
* **Forest Boundary:** The upper ridge and the lateral borders of the slope are tightly enclosed by a dense, green forest wall, creating a natural amphitheater and funneling the chaotic movement down the center.
* **The Run-Out Zone:** A flat, leveled area at the absolute base of the hill where gravity finally ceases its pull, allowing the tumbling bodies to slow down and come to a halt.

#### Activity and Human Pulse (The Race)
* **The Descent:** A frantic display of physical exertion and momentum. Participants (predominantly young men in casual and athletic wear) throw themselves down the nearly vertical drop. They rapidly lose their footing, resulting in spectacular, completely uncontrolled tumbling. Athletes are seen flipping limply through the air, sliding face-down on their chests, and suffering violent impacts against the uneven ground. Limbs are heavily blurred in motion, highlighting the extreme speed of the fall.
* **The Target Object:** A rapidly rolling, white-beige disc of cheese dictates the vector of the chase. It moves rapidly ahead of the falling participants, rolling down the green carpet of grass toward the bottom.
* **Preparation and Strategy:** At the very top edge of the hill, prior to the drop, participants display focused, tense energy. A young man in a green-red-black sports hoodie is visible gesturing decisively, indicating a path or strategy to the seated and standing competitors around him.

#### The Crowd, Aftermath, and Anomalies
* **The Spectators:** Hundreds of onlookers in multicolored everyday clothing form a dense, massive human barrier. They stand tightly packed along the tree lines on both the left and right sides of the slope, peering down the treacherous incline to observe the spectacle.
* **Triumph and Celebration:** At the bottom flat zone, the mood shifts to joyous relief. Participants who successfully reach the bottom celebrate intensely; one man in a dark navy jumpsuit is seen jumping high into the air with a raised fist, screaming in triumph, followed closely by another participant in a bright orange jumpsuit.
* **Consequences and Injuries:** The physical toll of the brutal descent is clearly visible in the aftermath. A barefoot young man in a blue-and-black shirt and grey shorts is seen relying heavily on a medical crutch, displaying clear signs of a foot or ankle injury sustained during the chaotic tumble.
* **Anomalies in the Crowd:** The flat zone contains eclectic human elements, including an individual dressed in a spotted Dalmatian costume and another person guiding a bicycle through the chaotic aftermath.
