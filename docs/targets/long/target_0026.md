# Target 0026

**Target coordinates:** 345r 3453    
**Date of the target:** July 17, 2026  

**Target:** Catastrophic flash flooding of the Guadalupe and Perdenales Rivers in central Texas - 29.794° N, 98.732° W (Boerne / Central Texas Region, USA)  

**Description:**
The target is a massive hydrological disaster unfolding across a rural and suburban landscape. A severe meteorological event has caused river systems to overflow, resulting in "large and deadly" flash floods that ravaged central Texas. The fast-moving, brown floodwaters inundated residential areas, engulfed roadways, swept away vehicles, and forced hundreds of emergency air and water rescues.

---

### Part 1: Meteorological and Hydrological Event

*   The region was battered by days of torrential rainstorms, generating a "record-shattering" amount of precipitation.
*   Forecasts indicated that up to 30 inches of total rain was expected to fall through the weekend, surpassing the precipitation levels of the previous year.
*   The intense rainfall triggered catastrophic flash flooding, notably causing the Guadalupe River to surge 32 feet (975 cm) in just a four-hour span.
*   The severe weather and rising waters impacted multiple counties across Texas, specifically including Uvalde, Kerr, and Kendall counties.

### Part 2: Visual and Kinetic Impact

*   **Inundated Roadways:** Visual data reveals rural highways and local roads completely washed out by raging, turbulent brown rapids, with the Perdenales River ravaging roads in Stonewall, Texas (image_3b3401.jpg, image_3b2fe6.jpg).
*   **Trapped Vehicles:** Cars are seen half-submerged and stranded in the deep, surging currents, with water reaching up to their windows (image_3b2c86.jpg, image_3b2f48.jpg).
*   **Wildlife Hazards:** The floodwaters were so deep and widespread that local wildlife, including a group of deer near Boerne, were caught in the drift and forced to stretch their heads above the treacherous currents to survive (image_3b2f6a.jpg).
*   **Debris:** The fast-moving water carried destructive debris, tearing down tree branches, sweeping away fences, and severely damaging local infrastructure (image_3b3401.jpg, image_3b2fe6.jpg).

### Part 3: Human Impact and Emergency Response

*   At the time of the reports, the sudden floods resulted in at least two fatalities, both occurring when the victims' vehicles were swept away by the intense river currents.
*   Emergency responders executed at least 230 rescues, utilizing boats and helicopters, and wading into the water to save trapped residents and carry small children to dry land.
*   In one instance, body camera footage captured a rescuer airlifting a girl to safety while her family and their dogs sheltered in their destroyed home waiting for a second helicopter.
*   The disaster struck the exact same area traumatized by flash floods the previous summer, an event that claimed over 130 lives, including 25 children and two counsellors at Camp Mystic near Kerrville.
