# Target 0015

**Target coordinates:** 5e8e 8383  
**Date of the target:** May 19, 2026  

**Target:** Eko-Myśl Waste Management Facility Operations (Dalsze, West Pomeranian Voivodeship, Poland). Municipal waste sorting, recycling, and disposal site.

**Description:**
The target is a vast, comprehensive municipal waste management and processing facility (MBP installation) isolated within a dense forest barrier. It encompasses a highly organized spatial layout featuring rigid industrial sorting halls, open-air biological processing basins, and massive excavated landfill pits. The site is characterized by the intense, continuous logistical movement of heavy machinery and transport trucks navigating across paved and unpaved surfaces. The facility exhibits a stark contrast between highly engineered mechanical recycling operations—such as tire shredding—and raw, open-air biological decomposition. The environment is heavily saturated with the severe odor of rotting biomass, drawing massive flocks of scavenging birds that circle above the active waste cells.

### Detailed Scene Breakdown

#### The Terrain and Natural Landscape
* **Landfill Cells:** Massive, excavated pits designed to hold post-production ballast and unrecoverable trash. These deep cells are separated by solid earthen berms and crisscrossed by a network of unpaved dirt roads used for technological access.
* **Forest Barrier:** A dense ring of green forest tightly surrounds the entire complex, serving as a natural visual and environmental buffer.
* **Public Road:** Route 23 functions as a massive, horizontal band of matter embedded in the ground, running along the outer perimeter and effectively enclosing the facility.

#### Architecture and Infrastructure
* **Processing Halls:** Rows of large, bright, rigid industrial buildings dedicated to mechanical-biological processing (MBP) and materials segregation. 
* **Biological Zones:** Visible from above are large, sealed concrete basins and cells. Some of these structures contain a dark liquid (leachate retention), presenting a cool, synchronized rhythm confined within artificial concrete boundaries, while others hold composting organic mass.
* **Paved Zones:** Expansive paved maneuvering yards, weighbridges, and designated parking areas exist to support the large fleet of heavy commercial vehicles.

#### Vehicles, Machinery, and Movement
* **Truck Logistics:** There is a continuous sequence of heavy-duty semi-trucks (notably featuring white cabs and large green trailers) moving purposefully through the facility. 
* **Active Dumping:** In a specific sector, a large truck is positioned on an elevated earthen ramp. Its green trailer is steeply tilted, actively dumping a massive load of mixed municipal waste down a raw slope. A human worker is present in the background, observing the process.
* **Compaction (Bulldozers):** At the base of the active landfill slope, powerful yellow compactors (bulldozers) are operating. They exhibit a constant, cold mechanical rhythm as they relentlessly spread and crush the freshly dumped waste into the ground to maximize volume efficiency.
* **Tire Shredding:** In a designated open-air sector lies a gigantic pile of used car tires. Immediately adjacent is a large, specialized orange mobile industrial shredder, continuously grinding the rigid rubber material into smaller fractions.

#### Environmental Conditions and Anomalies
* **Avian Activity:** Huge flocks of birds, primarily seagulls, form a chaotic, dynamic cloud circling and feeding directly over the open cells filled with fresh waste. 
* **Severe Odors:** The combination of active dumping, open-air composting, and the physical crushing of raw garbage generates an intense, highly unpleasant stench. The smell of decomposing biomass is a permanent, pervasive characteristic of the site, drifting heavily into the surrounding atmosphere.
