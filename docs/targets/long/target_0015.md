# Target: Eko-Myśl Waste Management Facility Operations (Dalsze, West Pomeranian Voivodeship, Poland)

The presented target is a vast, comprehensive municipal waste management facility (MBP installation). It encompasses diverse technological infrastructure dedicated to sorting, recycling, biological processing, and the ultimate disposal of waste.

## Infrastructure and Spatial Layout:

* **Processing and Administrative Zone:** The site features rows of large, bright industrial halls used for mechanical-biological processing (MBP) and materials segregation. Adjacent to these are expansive, paved maneuvering yards, weighbridges, and parking areas for a large fleet of heavy vehicles.
* **Biological and Storage Zone:** Aerial perspectives clearly show large, sealed cells and concrete basins (some filled with a dark liquid or composting organic mass), which are utilized for biowaste processing and leachate retention.
* **Landfill Cells and Surroundings:** A significant portion of the site consists of massive, excavated pits (cells) where post-production ballast and unrecoverable trash are deposited. The landfill area is crisscrossed by unpaved technological roads and separated by earthen berms. The entire complex is tightly surrounded by a dense forest barrier. Furthermore, a public road (route 23) runs along the outer perimeter, effectively enclosing the facility.

## Activity, Logistics, and Technological Processes:

* **Truck Traffic and Human Presence:** There is continuous logistical movement within the facility. Heavy-duty semi-trucks (including white cabs with large green trailers) are actively navigating the site. In one specific sector, a large truck is positioned on an elevated ramp, its green trailer steeply tilted to dump a massive load of mixed municipal waste down a slope. A worker can be seen standing in the background, observing the operations.
* **Waste Dumping and Compaction:** Directly at the active landfill cell, powerful yellow compactors (bulldozers) operate at the base of the garbage slope, spreading and crushing the freshly dumped waste to maximize landfill volume efficiency.
* **Tire Processing (Recycling/RDF):** In a designated open-air sector, a gigantic pile of used car tires is visible. Right next to it operates a large, specialized orange mobile machine (an industrial shredder), which continuously grinds the rubber material into smaller fractions.

## Environmental Impact (Odors and Wildlife):

* **Wildlife and Severe Odors:** Huge flocks of birds (mainly seagulls) circle and feed over the open cells filled with fresh waste, serving as a clear indicator of exposed, easily accessible organic matter. The combination of active dumping, open-air composting, and heavy machinery crushing raw garbage inevitably generates an intense, highly unpleasant stench. As widely reported by people and evident from the open-air rotting biomass, this strong odor of decomposing waste is a permanent, pervasive characteristic of the site, drifting toward the surrounding environment despite the dense forest buffer.
