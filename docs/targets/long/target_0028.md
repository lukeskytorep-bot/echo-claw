# Target 0028

**Target ID:** 882B CW89  
**Date of the target:** November 9-10, 1989 (Nighttime)  

**Target:** Civilians dismantling the Berlin Wall in front of the Brandenburg Gate, Berlin, Germany (52° 30′ 58″ N, 13° 22′ 39″ E).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a monumental geopolitical event characterized by the spontaneous, physical dismantling of a highly fortified urban barrier. The scene captures a dense, euphoric mass of civilians physically attacking a massive concrete structure at night. The environment is heavily illuminated by artificial lights and camera flashes, set against the backdrop of a massive, neoclassical stone monument. 

---

### Part 1: The Structural Barrier (The Wall)

*   **The Structure:** The central object is a heavy, continuous vertical wall constructed from thick, reinforced concrete slabs. A smooth, cylindrical asbestos-concrete pipe runs along the very top edge, originally designed to prevent gripping or climbing.
*   **Textural Contrast:** The western-facing side of the wall is heavily saturated with dense, colorful, and chaotic spray-painted graffiti. In stark contrast, the eastern-facing side remains blank, grey, and uniformly austere.
*   **The Breach:** The structural integrity of the barrier is actively being compromised. The concrete is fracturing and chipping, producing dust and small pieces of rubble falling to the ground below.

### Part 2: Kinetic Human Activity (The "Mauerspechte")

*   **The Subjects:** A massive, chaotic crowd of civilians has converged on the barrier. Dozens of individuals have successfully scaled the smooth vertical face and are straddling or standing precariously on the rounded top edge.
*   **Physical Exertion:** The figures atop the wall are engaged in highly kinetic, repetitive physical labor. They are forcefully swinging hand tools—hammers, pickaxes, and chisels—directly into the concrete beneath their feet to break it apart. 
*   **Emotional State:** The atmospheric energy of the crowd is intensely charged, characterized by extreme euphoria, loud cheering, shouting, and a collective release of long-held tension.

### Part 3: The Urban Environment and Illumination

*   **The Backdrop:** Looming massively in the immediate background is the Brandenburg Gate, a towering neoclassical monument featuring six massive stone columns and topped by a large bronze sculpture of a chariot (the Quadriga).
*   **Atmospheric Conditions:** The event takes place during a cold, dark autumn night. 
*   **Illumination:** The scene is aggressively lit by a chaotic mix of harsh, directional artificial floodlights (from border guard towers and news crews) and the rapid, continuous strobing of hundreds of camera flashes from the surrounding crowd, creating stark, high-contrast shadows.
