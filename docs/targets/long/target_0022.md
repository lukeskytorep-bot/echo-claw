# Target 0022

**Target coordinates:**  
**Date of the target:** 19 July 2026  

**Target:** The North Pole Marathon conducted on a drifting, temporary sheet of sea ice. (90° 00′ 00″ N (Geographic North Pole) )

**Description:**
The target is a high-endurance biological event taking place in a highly hostile, ephemeral, and remote geographic environment. The scene involves 23 human subjects running a designated circular route directly on the frozen surface of the Arctic Ocean. The event is characterized by the stark contrast between human physical exertion and the barren, flat, and frozen landscape. The entire operational area is temporary; the structural integrity of the "ground" is actively shifting and the course disappeared into the ocean just hours after the event concluded.

---

### Part 1: The Environment and The Vessel

*   **The Surface:** The terrain is a flat, expansive sheet of white, drifting sea ice floating over the deep Arctic Ocean. The weather conditions are overcast and foggy, creating a low-visibility, monochromatic white and grey environment. Dark, open ocean water is visible at the edge of the ice sheet where it fractures.
*   **The Basecamp (Le Commandant Charcot):** A massive French polar expedition ship is moored directly against the edge of the sea ice. The vessel has a dark blue/black lower hull with a distinct red stripe, and a large, multi-deck white superstructure featuring a helipad on the bow. The ship serves as the delivery mechanism and safe haven, dwarfing the human subjects on the ice below.

### Part 2: Biological Activity and Unique Constraints

*   **The Runners:** Competitors from seven different countries are navigating the frozen terrain. Due to the extreme environmental hazards, they exhibit a highly unusual visual profile for marathon runners: they are dressed in heavy, multi-layered thermal clothing, snow goggles or sunglasses, and mandatory bright red inflatable life vests worn over their gear (a precaution against the ice cracking and plunging them into the freezing ocean).
*   **Security Perimeter:** The environment is actively hostile. In addition to the shifting ice, armed guards physically patrol the perimeter of the running course, keeping a continuous watch for apex predators (polar bears) that pose a real, immediate threat to the runners. 

### Part 3: The Race Infrastructure

*   **The Course:** The track is a 1,055-metre loop carved into the snow and ice. Small marker flags delineate the path. A wide aerial view shows the runners spread out in a single-file line traversing the vast, featureless white expanse.
*   **Start/Finish Line:** The focal point of the infrastructure is a pair of large, black, angled inflatable arches acting as the start and finish line. A black banner reading "NORTH POLE MARATHON SUMMER 2026" is stretched across the gap. 
*   **Support Stations:** Makeshift tables are set up directly on the ice to provide sustenance. Close-up visual data shows large metal bowls filled with fresh red, green, and yellow apples, as well as dried snacks, providing a burst of vivid natural color against the stark, frozen background.
