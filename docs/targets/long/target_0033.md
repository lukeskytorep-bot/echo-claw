# Target 0033

**Target ID:** 681C FS12  
**Date of the target:** February 21, 2012 (approx. 7:15 a.m. local time)  

**Target:** The controlled demolition of the Fort Steuben Bridge over the Ohio River, located between Steubenville, Ohio, and Weirton, West Virginia (40.3798° N, 80.6133° W).

**Description:**
The target is a factual profile of a controlled, highly coordinated structural demolition of an aging suspension bridge. The scene captures the exact millisecond of explosive detonation, characterized by a massive, blinding horizontal line of fire severing the steel structure, set against a calm, dim river valley at dawn.

---

### Part 1: The Infrastructure and Environment

*   **The Structure:** The main object is an 83-year-old wire-cable suspension bridge. It features a 1,255-foot suspended span supported by two prominent, vertical steel lattice towers resting on concrete piers. 
*   **The Landscape:** The surrounding environment is a natural river valley bordered by sloping, wooded hills. The event takes place in the early morning, characterized by dim, muted ambient light and cold winter conditions.
*   **The Waterway:** Directly beneath the structure is the wide, flat expanse of the Ohio River. The water is relatively calm, acting as a large, dark reflective surface for the events occurring above.

### Part 2: The Demolition and Kinetic Energy

*   **The Explosives:** The absolute center of kinetic change is the synchronized detonation of 490 strategically placed shaped charges (utilizing 153 pounds of explosives mounted in 136 locations).
*   **The Flash:** The visual data captures a continuous, brilliant horizontal chain of bright orange and yellow explosive flashes stretching entirely across the bridge's main deck. The sheer intensity of the light completely illuminates the surrounding water and shoreline.
*   **Secondary Blasts:** Simultaneously, isolated and highly concentrated explosive blasts are detonating at critical structural points on the upper sections of the vertical steel lattice towers.

### Part 3: Structural Failure and Gravity

*   **The Cut:** The engineered purpose of the shaped charges is to act as a massive remote torch, instantly cutting through the rigid steel superstructure in a matter of milliseconds.
*   **The Collapse:** The intense release of energy causes immediate structural failure. As the wire cables and stiffening trusses are severed, the mechanical tension is broken. 
*   **The Trajectory:** This engineered loss of tension forces the two main steel towers to lose vertical stability and begin rotating outward toward their respective shorelines. This ensures the fragmented bridge deck drops cleanly into the river below for subsequent debris removal.
